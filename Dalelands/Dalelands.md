A new campaign set in the Dalelands, right after the events of [[Descent into Avernus]]. The party meets in [[Shadowdale]] and takes up a quest from a knight of [[Torm]]. Eventually our heroes will cross paths with [[Ira Ferox - Josh]] and company.

## Campaign Log
- [[Session 1 - To New Beginnings]]: the vision, the [[Old Skull Inn]], and the [[The Broken Tower Bridge]].
- [[Session 2]]: we free [[Adon Twirtho]], learn the Ruehs held him, and fight their mercenaries in the far tower with the siblings on our side. We stop for the night as the last mercenary yields.

## The Party
- [[Warden Morn Ingwood]] (Josh): wood elf Twilight cleric of [[Selûne]]
- [[Brok Digginstone]]: dwarf bard
- [[Havelin Alderhilts]]: elf ranger
- [[Teager]]: elf wizard
- Patron: [[Offsha Di'aylen]], knight of [[Torm]]
