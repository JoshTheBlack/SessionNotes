The militant arm of the Church of [[Selûne]]. [[Warden Morn Ingwood]] is a loosely affiliated member, and wears their silver crescent.

> [!info]- Lore
> The Silverstars are [[Selûne]]'s holy order, dedicated to protecting people from creatures of the dark.

> [!warning]- A note on the name
> In Realms canon the "Silverstars" or "Moonstars" are Khelben Blackstaff's secretive group. Morn's character sheet uses the name for the militant order of [[Selûne]]'s church, so this is **our table's** Silverstars.
