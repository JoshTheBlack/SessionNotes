A knight in plate mail, a devotee of [[Torm]] from a minor noble family in Flan. He recently came from [[Elturel]] on a mission from [[Torm]] to return to [[Tantras]], and is gathering adventurers to find out why priests of [[Torm]] are vanishing. He gave up his family's trade for the path of righteousness.

## The Lookout
He walked with us to [[The Broken Tower Bridge]] and stayed up top as our lookout while we went below. When we had not returned, he followed our trail down and found us just as we reached [[Adon Twirtho]]. We sent him back up to the surface with Adon.

## Session 2
He never came back down. We never saw how, but he crossed the creek on the surface and burst in through the exterior door of the far tower, shouting something about evildoers, just as the battle began. It was the only way he could have reached that door. He fell during the fight, and [[Warden Morn Ingwood]] pulled him back up with a [[Healing Word]]. Whatever else he is, he is brave to the point of recklessness, and he arrived at the one moment when everyone in the room was holding their breath.

> [!question] Open questions
> - What does he make of [[Mardon Rueh]] and [[Wendy Rueh]], exiles from [[Baldur's Gate]] who fought beside us? A knight of [[Torm]] who calls strangers evildoers on sight will have opinions.
