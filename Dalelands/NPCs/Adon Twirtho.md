[[Rolf Twirtho]]'s brother. We found him behind bars in a room off the tunnel beneath [[The Broken Tower Bridge]], near the far tower, east of [[Shadowdale]]. He was held by [[Mardon Rueh]] and [[Wendy Rueh]].

## Rescue
In [[Session 2]], [[Brok Digginstone]] asked the man in the cell who he was, and he gave his name as Adon. We told him we had come to rescue him. After a brief conversation to verify he was who he said he was, we broke him out of his cell. [[Offsha Di'aylen]] caught up with us about then, and we left the two of them on the surface while we pressed on.

> [!info] Why he was taken
> The Ruehs held him as part of some scheme to raise money, to help them press a campaign to reinstate their family name in [[Baldur's Gate]]. We don't have the details, but they did not harm him.
