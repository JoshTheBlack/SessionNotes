A well-kept man of aristocratic bearing, met in the foyer of the far tower beyond [[The Broken Tower Bridge]] in [[Session 2]]. He and his sister, [[Wendy Rueh]], are from [[Baldur's Gate]]. Another family ruined their name, and they fled the city. Warden Morn does not trust soft-handed city folk, but when the blades came out, Mardon fought on our side against his own mercenaries.

## What I Know
- He and his sister held [[Adon Twirtho]] in the cell beneath the tower, as part of a scheme to raise money for a campaign to reinstate their family name.
- Their wealthy aristocratic family fled [[Baldur's Gate]] after rumors spread that they were devil worshippers.
- Asked who we were when he and his sister stepped out of a side door in the foyer.
- Commanded his mercenaries to leave. They refused to go without payment.
- We convinced him that his best path forward was to come and face justice for the kidnapping. We promised to advise the authorities to be lenient, because Adon was not harmed.
- When [[Offsha Di'aylen]] burst in and the fight started, he stood and fought with us against the men he had hired.
- Kept telling his sister to stay out of the fight.

> [!info]- Where he comes from
> The nobility of [[Baldur's Gate]] were collectively called the patriar families, the old aristocracy who sat alongside the merchants and mercenary captains who really ran the city. A fallen patriar family fleeing the city is not unusual.

> [!question] Open questions
> - Which family spread the rumors, and were they true?
> - Who among the nobles of [[Baldur's Gate]] wanted their name ruined, and why would a rumor of devil worship stick?
> - Where do the two stairways at the halfway point of the tunnel lead? Ask him.
> - We promised to ask the authorities for leniency. Which authorities, and will they listen?
