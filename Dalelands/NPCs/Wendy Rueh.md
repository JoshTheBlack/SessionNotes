Sister of [[Mardon Rueh]], and, as far as I can tell, a priest of [[Oghma]]. She and her brother are exiles from [[Baldur's Gate]], where their family's name was ruined by rumors of devil worship. In the fight in [[Session 2]] she stood on our side against her own mercenaries. She stayed behind us, offering healing now and then.

## What I Know
- Appeared in the foyer with her brother and listened while we negotiated.
- While we tried to pry information from her, she muttered, "We aren't devil worshippers." Nobody had accused her.
- Wears the faith of [[Oghma]], the Binder, god of knowledge.
- In the fight, her brother kept telling her to stay out of it. She kept saying "but I can help" and following along, healing [[Warden Morn Ingwood]] after a blade had put him down. A healer who helps a stranger in the middle of a brawl is hard to hate.

> [!info]- Her god
> Followers of [[Oghma]] are told to gather, preserve and spread knowledge, to oppose liars and trickery, and never to harm a singer or bard, so [[Brok Digginstone]] may have a friend here.

> [!question] Open questions
> - What is she doing so far from any library or temple of [[Oghma]]?
> - What did she and her brother plan to do with [[Adon Twirtho]]?
> - Were the rumors true?
