The inn in [[Shadowdale]] where [[Warden Morn Ingwood]] met his party. Morn sleeps on the roof.

> [!info]- Lore
> The Old Skull Inn is a long-standing landmark of Shadowdale in the Forgotten Realms, a rumor mill the locals treat like a town hall.

> [!info]- More Realms lore (Forgotten Realms Wiki)
> Built in 1280 DR by Buldo Silvermane on the ruins of the Twisted Tower Inn, owned in later years by Jhaele Silvermane and then his son Durgo. Three floors and a cellar. Famous for its honest staff, good food and safety in the middle of the dale, so much so that "oldskull" became a term for an excellent place to drink. Rumor holds that secret passages below the inn lead to Old Skull Tor and into the Underdark. (Realms canon, which may differ from our table's version.)
