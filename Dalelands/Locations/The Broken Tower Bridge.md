A ruined tower bridge three hours from [[Shadowdale]], where the creek is crossed by laid logs now. Cultists in leather and scimitars held the near tower, and [[Mardon Rueh]] and [[Wendy Rueh]] held the far one, along with [[Adon Twirtho]] in the dungeon below, under the creek. First dungeon of the campaign.

## Layout So Far
1. **The near tower.** Huge cracks in the walls, wide enough to walk through. Three men in leather armor with scimitars and small bows. We cleared it in [[Session 1 - To New Beginnings]]. [[Offsha Di'aylen]] stayed up here as our lookout.
2. **The stairs down.** Lead to hallways of rooms, apparently empty. [[Brok Digginstone]] found the party's first treasure there (see the [[Dalelands Party Inventory]]).
3. **The long tunnel under the creek.** Runs from the near tower beneath the creek to the far tower. A room off the tunnel near the far tower holds the cell where we found [[Adon Twirtho]].
4. **Two stairways down.** At the halfway point, between the two towers, two stairways lead further down. Unexplored. Ask the Ruehs.
5. **The far stairs.** Near the foot of the staircase leading up, two rooms with bedding pallets. Just bedrolls, and nothing alive in either.
6. **The creaky doors.** At the top of the stairs, a large set of wooden double doors that creak loudly, with a second set behind them.
7. **The foyer.** The main floor of the far tower, an odd-shaped room with five more doors and an exterior entrance. Voices behind two. The Ruehs stepped out of one of them, and the fight with their mercenaries happened here in [[Session 2]]. [[Offsha Di'aylen]] burst in through the exterior door after crossing the creek on the surface.
