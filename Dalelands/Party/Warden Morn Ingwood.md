### Wood Elf Twilight Cleric of Selûne

*Played by Josh.* The Warden of the Deep Woods, born in [[Highmoon]] and raised by the [[Cormanthor]]. A solitary ranger for decades until a blizzard and a moonbeam made him a cleric of [[Selûne]]. He's a hundred and fifteen, bright green eyes, dark brown hair tied in a working knot, and a coppery, leathered look. Nocturnal by nature: given the choice he'll sleep on the roof of an inn rather than under it.

He carries a chunk of pale quartz that glows with soft silver light when the moon is hidden. When on watch, he twists and reassembles a layered wooden puzzle.

**Ideals:** Be the light in the dark. [[Selûne]]'s moonlight guides travelers home, and so must he.
**Bonds:** The ancient elven timberlands of [[Deepingdale]]. Awful visions of a coming disaster.
**Flaws:** Slow to trust soft-handed city dwellers and other races. Wealth and manners won't save you from a hungry owlbear.
**Allies:** Priestess Elara Moonsong (wandering Selûnite who taught him his Twilight gifts). Sergeant Therren of the Highmoon Watch, who leaves rations for him at a hollow stump.
**Enemies:** The Cult of [[Shar]], and Underdark things that crawl up from below: phase spiders, grimlocks.
**Organization:** [[The Silverstars]], the militant arm of [[Selûne]]'s church.

> [!quote]- How I tell it
> *Morn narrates in a low, watchful voice. Reverent and quiet, he talks in terms of light and shadow, flock and shepherd, wolves and owls. He doesn't trust a roof or a rich man, and he trusts his party more than he lets on.*
