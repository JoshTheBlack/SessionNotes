A dwarven bard who was playing bagpipes in the [[Old Skull Inn]] when [[Warden Morn Ingwood]] first saw him in a moon-vision. Quick with a coin pouch: he found the loot in the tower dungeon.

In [[Session 2]] he was the first to speak to [[Adon Twirtho]] through the bars of his cell, and he slew the dog in the fight with the mercenaries. A follower of [[Oghma]] is told never to harm a singer or a bard, so [[Wendy Rueh]] ought to think twice.
