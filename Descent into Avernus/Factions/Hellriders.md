 Paladins sworn to protect [[Elturel]].  It has been speculated that they fought their way into Hell itself.

#factions #Elturel

We met their dead in the crypts of [[Avernus]], and their last survivors in [[Olanthius]], [[Harroman]] and [[Yale]]. Their story is in [[Mission 4 - Journey to Candlekeep]] and [[Mission 7 - Where Demons are Created]].

> [!info]- Lore
> The Hellriders were Elturel's paladin knights who followed Zariel into Avernus to fight the Blood War. When she fell, some fell with her, and some died for her cause.
