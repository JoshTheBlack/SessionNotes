A group of vagabonds and murderers who were haunted by an ancient crime that cost them their wings.  They live on the edge of human society.

> [!info]- From the book
> The Kenku Killers come from the book's character-background hook "a night's worth of earnings from the [[The Low Lantern|Low Lantern]]," in the heist table: *you didn't think you left any loose ends, but now a gang of kenku killers haunts your trail and your nightmares.* Their theft consequences (the owner got the authorities involved, another thieves' group is after you) match the [[The Flaming Fist|Flaming Fist]] reward in [[Campaign Background]].
