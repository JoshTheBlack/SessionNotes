The codex of every [[Elturel]] citizen who swore the oath to [[Elturgard]], in one hand across the ages. [[Pherria Jynks]] guards it.

> [!info]- More from the book
> The *Tome of the Creed Resolute* is kept in the crypts beneath [[High Hall]]. Its first page carries the oath every citizen of Elturgard takes when old enough to read, swearing to defend Elturel body and soul. The rest is signatures, all apparently in the same hand.
