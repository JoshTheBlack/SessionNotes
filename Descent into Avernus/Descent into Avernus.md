> [!quote] Ira Ferox
> *Gruff, brash, and quick to anger. Distrusts anyone with a badge, a crown or a sermon. Fiercely devoted to his own skin and to second helpings.*

A campaign of *Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus* played from 1st level to the end. The party, [[==Umbra Contundens==]], begins as five old thieves hauled back into service by the [[The Flaming Fist]] when [[Elturel]] vanishes, and ends up following the trail of a hellish bargain all the way into [[Avernus]].
