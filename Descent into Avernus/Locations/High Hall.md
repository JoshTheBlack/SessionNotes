The cathedral of [[Torm]] in [[Elturel]], half burned and crawling with devils. Beneath it, catacombs full of refugees, a hidden codex, and a [[Holy Avenger]] nobody noticed.

> [!info]- Lore
> High Hall, the Hall High Cathedral, is the heart of [[Torm]]'s faith in Elturel and holds the catacombs where survivors hide.

> [!quote]- From the book (ch. 2)
> *Situated atop this balcony is a magnificent pipe organ. Its ivory keys practically glow, even as its ebony keys seem to absorb all light.*

> [!info]- More from the book
> The devils defaced the holy altar of [[Torm]]. Any creature that doesn't worship or swear fealty to [[Zariel]] and starts its turn within 20 ft gains vulnerability to all damage. It can be cleansed by kneeling, praying to [[Torm]] for a minute, and succeeding on a DC 15 Intelligence (Religion) check. An escape tunnel from the crypts ends at a hole in the floating earthmote Elturel clings to, about 500 ft above the River Styx.
