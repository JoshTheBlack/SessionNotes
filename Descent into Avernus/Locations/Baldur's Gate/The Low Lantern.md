The **Low Lantern**, owned and operated by [[Amrik Vanthampur]], is a converted three-mast ship, used as a festhall, tavern, and gambling house in [[Baldur's Gate]].  It was often used as a place for covert meetings.

> [!info]- From the book
> An aging three-masted merchant ship permanently docked on the east side of the harbor, converted into a tavern and gambling house that never closes. A green lantern at the bow means it's open. Owner: [[Laraelra Thundreth]]. Staff: two kenku bartenders and six bouncers, in three eight-hour shifts. The ravens in the crow's nest are two of [[Thurstwell Vanthampur]]'s imps, spying on [[Amrik Vanthampur]]. They kill any seagull that wanders past, which is why the decks are strewn with dead gulls (a DC 13 Medicine check shows they were stung).
