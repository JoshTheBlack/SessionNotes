> [!info]- From the book
> The pirate ship of Captain [[Murosko Sessprin (Captain)|Sessprin]], later seized by Dead-Eye. It's moored at the end of a dock on the east side of the harbor, guarded by eleven bandits, with no treasure aboard. It needs a crew of twenty (half with water vehicle proficiency) to sail and is worth 10,000 gp intact, but nobody in [[Baldur's Gate]] wants to buy it because of its reputation. That made it a perfect party base.
