The front for the [[Dead Three]] cult in [[Baldur's Gate]]: a stucco building with a clay-tile roof, nymphs carved into the gates, shallow pools and massage rooms upstairs, and a flooded dungeon of shrines, torture chambers and hags beneath it. Owned by the [[Thalamra Vanthampur]] family and run day-to-day by [[Mortlock Vanthampur]]. The story is in [[Mission 1b - The Acolyte's Hideout]].
