The front for the [[Dead Three]] cult in [[Baldur's Gate]]: a stucco building with a clay-tile roof, nymphs carved into the gates, shallow pools and massage rooms upstairs, and a flooded dungeon of shrines, torture chambers and hags beneath it. Owned by the [[Thalamra Vanthampur]] family and run day-to-day by [[Mortlock Vanthampur]]. The story is in [[Mission 2 - The Acolyte's Hideout]].

> [!info]- From the book
> A public bathhouse northwest of the Elfsong, with a walled garden and frolicking nymphs carved into its gates. It hides the Dungeon of the Dead Three. After the dungeon is cleared, cultists of Tiamat led by a cult fanatic named Ultiss (spelled Ultus in our first notes) attack, sent by Arkhan the Cruel to recover the treasure stolen from Tiamat's hoard on [[Avernus]], which [[Zariel]]'s agents gave to the Vanthampurs to buy the Dead Three's loyalty.
