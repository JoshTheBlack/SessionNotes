A tavern located just inside the gate to Wyrm's Crossing on the eastern side of the lower city.
So named because of a ghostly spirit that occasionally sings a love ballad in elvish.

> [!info]- Lore
> A famous [[Baldur's Gate]] tavern whose ghostly elven song and floating-beholder sign draw regulars. In the module, the Elfsong is where the characters meet [[Tarina (Spy)|Tarina]] and are pointed toward the [[Dead Three]].

> [!info]- More from the book
> Run by [[Alan Alyth]], with cooks Chenna Fatrabbit, Azar Valsheem and Klav Martilmur. Specialties: fish cakes, crab cakes, thick cheese-and-potato soup, and loaf pudding soaked in syrup with salted almonds. A haunting elf spirit sings the ballad that gives the place its name. A shipwright named Skrawldar Fane is a regular. The sahuagin priestess [[Oshalla]] rents the large guest room.
