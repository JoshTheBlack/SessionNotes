A junkyard fort in [[Avernus]], run by [[Mad Maggie]] and kept running by [[Chukka]] and [[Clonk]]. Guarded by heckling gnomes, a password ("knuckle sandwich"), and a secret dance (the worm).

> [!info]- Lore
> A walled outpost of scavenged infernal machines where [[Lulu]]'s memories are restored. The gnomes at the gate demand a password and a dance.
