An alabaster temple buried in a hill-sized scab in a swamp of blood, bound with black iron chains. Inside, tunnels of flesh, hellish things, and a cathedral with [[Zariel]]'s sword in the dais. The [[Hellriders]]' ghost [[Yale]] built it to protect the blade.

> [!info]- Lore
> The citadel exists to hide the Sword of Zariel. The scab, grown by Avernus itself, is the demons' wound on the world.
