An alabaster temple buried in a hill-sized scab in a swamp of blood, bound with black iron chains. Inside, tunnels of flesh, hellish things, and a cathedral with [[Zariel]]'s sword in the dais. The [[Hellriders]]' ghost [[Yael]] built it to protect the blade.

> [!info]- Lore
> The citadel exists to hide the [[Sword of Zariel]]. The scab, grown by [[Avernus]] itself, is the demons' wound on the world.

> [!quote]- From the book (ch. 4)
> *A bright white light burns away the blood and grit staining your clothes. Restorative energy brings life to numb muscles as the glow softens to reveal the interior of a sun-kissed cathedral. How light passes through the scab and into the stained glass windows is a mystery only magic can answer.*
>
