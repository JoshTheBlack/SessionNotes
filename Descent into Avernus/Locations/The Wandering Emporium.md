A traveling market in the [[Avernus]] wastes, ringed with demon grinders and run by [[Mahadi The Rakshasa]].

- [[Infernal Rapture]] is the tavern.
- Firesnake Forge buys and sells weapons and armor. We sold our spares there for 10 soul coins.
- Burnie the Barber: you forget everything that happens inside.
- From Here to Avernus is the post office.
- Ichors Away is an apothecary of ichor.
- Zeneth's Larva Farm keeps six large larvae in cages.

> [!info]- Lore
> The Wandering Emporium visits all corners of Avernus. Payment is due at time of service; alternative payment is available.
