The Vanthampur family estate in the Upper City of [[Baldur's Gate]]: gilded halls on top, a sewer cult and a devil shrine on the bottom. Home of [[Thalamra Vanthampur]], [[Thurstwell Vanthampur]] and a flock of invisible imps. We came for a ransom. We left with a puzzle box, a shield, three captives, and the Duke's corpse.
