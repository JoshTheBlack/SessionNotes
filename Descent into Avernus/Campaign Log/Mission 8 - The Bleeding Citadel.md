> [!abstract] The Job
> Get into [[The Bleeding Citadel]] and claim [[Zariel]]'s sword.

## The Scab
With our freshly marked map leading the way, we head for the Bleeding Citadel. A few hours later we arrive. A great, disgusting scab the size of a hill rises from a stinking swamp of blood. The domed top of an alabaster temple pokes up through it. Black iron chains converge on it from every direction.

We try to scale the chains. [[Thomus - Thomas]] and I make it. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]], [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] and [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah]] fall into the clotted moat and have to swim out. We help them up. Inside is a cavernous system of fleshy tunnels.

On one side, the scab has been stripped from the passage, revealing a stained glass window of a contemplative angel in opaque blues and yellows. We can't open it, so we go on, up the rope and down the fleshy tunnel.

## The Tunnels
A junction leads down to a room with five goat-headed demons around a pool of blood. Mike hits two with a lightning bolt and I rush in screaming. Four swings and one lies at my feet. Four more and another. I kick them into the blood pool. Thomas throws some lightning, and Zaleria crits one with a guiding bolt and steals one of my kills. We finish the rest. In the pool floats a corpse with a flask of water from the river Styx and a diamond worth 5,000 gp.

Further on is a 40-foot-wide chamber with a **weeping wound**: a gash in the ceiling leaks blood into a pool where a scab floats. Baktha walks in and is ambushed by four invisible barlguras, each popping into view as it drops down. Baktha and Mike land some damage. I step in and kill two, then another, then the last. Baktha points out the barlguras were ripping the wound open, so Thomas casts healing on it and the bleeding stems.

We drop down another level into a big empty chamber with a lake of blood. At the far tunnel, two frog-like aberrations fight over a corpse. We try to sneak in, quite ungracefully, and provoke them into a battle, which we win handily with haste. The corpse has a **+1 glaive**. We take it.

Heading back east under the stained glass window, we find a chamber of treasure that looks like a night hag's hoard.

Deeper: ten small, snarling demons clawing at the rock floor, with a cloud of flies shaped like a demon barking orders at them. I drop in with a plunging attack on two. The fly-cloud demon disappears into the wall. Zaleria, Mike and Thomas fire spells down at them. Baktha eventually drops in and kills two. They swarm me and fail to land a hit. I kill four. Zaleria drops more guiding bolts, Thomas follows suit, Mike drops a starry wisp, Baktha bashes another. I slice the last two into ribbons.

Another tunnel opens into a massive chamber where horse-sized flies are eating a spiny devil hung by chains from the ceiling. I charge in and fire a lightning bolt from my sword. They fly at me and wound me grievously. Thomas runs in and heals me. The rest filter in. One drops Baktha with its droning hum. I slay it with four blows, drop another a few seconds later, and Thomas drops the last after I weaken it.

The devil hanging from the chains is **Alazub**. He trusts Lulu. He came to the scab looking for [[Trantalox]], a nalfeshnee after Zariel's sword. Kroteqnoeque is leading a crew of demons trying to dig into the citadel, and Alazub wants to stop them. We free him since our goals line up, and he's grateful.

Back in the chamber where Kroteqnoeque's goons dug a week ago, we glimpse demons heading the same way. They notice us and run. We give chase and lose them.

## The Doors
We drop down and take the left path to a massive chamber with brass double doors bearing a relief of Zariel, with gold runes inlaid in the frame. Three barb-headed demons are bashing the door while another shouts at them in Abyssal. Mike casts feather fall on us all and we drop in.

I turn one into a cloud of blood mist, 16 pieces in an instant. Baktha and Zaleria press. Thomas and Mike send balls of holy energy. After a few rounds we stand surveying the wounds among their corpses. Baktha is hurt badly. Then Zaleria pushes the door they couldn't break, and it swings open with a slight push.

## The Cathedral
A bright white light burns the blood and grit from our clothes. Restorative energy wakes numb muscles as the glow softens to reveal a sun-kissed cathedral. How light passes through the scab and into the stained glass is a mystery only magic can answer. Pillars line a path to a raised dais carved with celestial runes. **Zariel's sword is embedded in the dais.**

When we step in, the room vanishes and we stand in **Idleglen** with a 30-foot statue of Zariel on a dais. Gnolls surround us. We kill them, judiciously. Then dretches. We kill them *extra* judiciously. A bloodied woman crawls out from under a toppled cart, grateful. A mastiff and a giant hyena fight ahead; we kill the hyena and the mastiff is grateful. A serpent-and-gnoll demon chases a group of people. We hurt it, the people flee, it flees. Five human guards splattered with black ichor advance on us.

I slip around the statue and attack the demon bashing at the door of a ruined building. A group cowers behind it. Behind me I hear the others fighting the guards. A bright flash and a blast of heat is punctuated by a deadly scream. I don't recognize the voice.

Baktha finishes the demon and we call to the people inside. They let us in. After some convincing, the priest, Jessa, steps outside to restore the guards. I follow her out to stand guard. She restores them, lifting whatever curse controlled them. Our minds fill with an unfamiliar woman's voice: we have one hour before the really bad stuff begins. We use it to rest.

A noise wakes us. We step outside, telling the survivors to bar the door. Six demonic dog-like figures jog behind a large gorilla-like demon and an even bigger dog-like demon. A long, vicious battle ensues and just as we're about to finish it, **Yeenoghu** charges out of the fog.

We trade blows with the demon lord for a few rounds. Then Zariel and Lulu, in their former, pre-fallen forms, arrive and shove Yeenoghu through a portal. Zariel asks each of us the same question: *would you save everyone if it meant sacrificing yourself?* Only Baktha says yes. She gives him a **charm of vitality**, and gives Thomas and Zaleria a **charm of restoration**. Mike and I answered no. We get nothing. I'd say it stung, but I'm too busy not caring.

They disappear through the portal, and we're pulled into the pure white void of the sword chamber. Lulu, in her current form, is here, along with one of Zariel's riders, [[Yale]]. Lulu says Zariel had to give her soul, which turned her into a demon. They've kept her sword in the hope that it could restore her.

Yale asks which of us will take the sword and cease to be. Thomas volunteers and grabs it. As he pulls it out, a burst explodes from where we stand, destroying everything near. The shield on my back screeches, trying to overthrow my will, and fails.

Thomas is transformed into a lawful good, goody two-shoes.
