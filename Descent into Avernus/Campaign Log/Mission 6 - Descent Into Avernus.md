> [!abstract] The Job
> Find [[Zariel]]'s sword before whoever else is hunting for it does. First stop: [[Fort Knucklebone]], where [[Lulu]] says she can get her memories back.

## The Fall
We dive off the edge of [[Elturel]], aiming for [[Fort Knucklebone]]. Landmarks announce themselves as we pass, whispered by the map straight into [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]]'s mind.

We land. The air smells like burning tar. The wind alternates between gale and soft breeze. Ash chokes the air, and giant flies are everywhere. Nupperibos crawl on the ground. Puddles of demon ichor dot the wasteland like the world's worst picnic.

We pick a direction and walk. The first few hours are mercifully quiet.

## The Wandering Emporium
Then, off to the side, Lulu points out [[The Wandering Emporium]], ringed with a perimeter of demon grinders. Inside, we meet its proprietor, [[Mahadi The Rakshasa]], who recommends the [[Infernal Rapture]] if we're hungry. Payment due at time of service. Alternative payment available. I do not like the sound of "alternative."

He offers to sell us an infernal machine for roughly 10 soul coins, about 1,000 gold. It runs on soul coins.

Stalls inside the Emporium:
- [[Infernal Rapture]]: a tavern.
- Firesnake Forge: weapons and armor. We sell a pile of spare gear for 10 soul coins.
- Burnie the Barber: a barber shop where you forget everything that happened after you leave. No thanks.
- From Here to Avernus: a post office.
- Ichors Away: an apothecary of ichor.
- Zeneth's Larva Farm: six large larvae in cages.

[[Thomus - Thomas]] spends the visit trying to piss off every local by asking each why they're sentenced to Hell. At the Infernal Rapture, a tall, grim, hook-nosed humanoid with spindly fingers stands at a podium and asks if we'd like to come in. The prices are exorbitant. Baktha and [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] order dessert. Thomas orders tea. I order *nothing*, and it kills me.

## The Wasteland
We walk until we can't, then camp. After a rest, we move on. The wind shifts to our tails and four lemures attack. We dispatch them and arrive at [[Fort Knucklebone]].

## Fort Knucklebone
Gnomish creatures stand sentry on the walls and heckle us for the password. I tell them it's *knuckle sandwich*. They want a secret dance. Baktha does the worm. They laugh, open the gate, and let us in.

Inside are [[Chucka]] and [[Clonk]]. They bring out an old hag with red-eyed ravens on her shoulders and a giant fleshy golem trailing behind: [[Mad Maggie]]. She says she can help Lulu recover her memories and goes off to prepare a ritual.

Chucka and Clonk are struggling to fix a demon grinder. Mike studies it and finds a gear suffering from a demonic energy malady. Baktha casts a spell and the machine whirs back to life. Clonk dashes off to tell Maggie we're useful.

We meet the golem, hurt. Lulu discerns his name: Mickey. Thomas studies his wounds and heals him up a bit.

An impish interloper tells us Maggie's ready. He turns into a raven and we follow him to her quarters.

## The Ritual
The ritual pulls us into a dream. We push through the first level and end up dream-fighting devils. When we beat them, they beg us to let them live, and they'll serve us for eternity. We spare them.

Then we wander a vast black landscape of devastation, overwhelmed with loss. Maggie's disembodied voice: "Don't let the sludge consume you, you must push through her defenses. You must *look*." The black goop begins to envelop us. Baktha breaks free first and floats in the air. The devils we spared return just as I break loose, taking psychic damage as despair tries to drown me. Thomas follows, then Salah, and finally Mike, with an assist from the devils.

The next vision: Lulu approaches a spyglass, presses her eye to it, steps back, and cries out that she knows where the sword is. We all wake.

Lulu is ecstatic. She is ripped and ready. Maggie seems deeply contented, happy to have shared that suffering with us, and rewards us with gifts. We rest and set off, promising to return and show her the sword if we can.

## The Demon Grinder
An hour out from the fort, a big red dust cloud barrels toward us, ridden by boar-headed and rat-headed humanoids. It's an infernal machine. Baktha charges straight at it, turns into mist at the end of his movement, solidifies and shouts "HALT!" The machine screeches to a stop, catapulting three wereboars and two wererats to the ground. Only the driver stays seated, dazed.

I rush the biggest and send a lightning bolt from my sword. Thomas fireballs everything on the ground. Mike finishes the big one with three scorching rays. The wererats get to Baktha and one takes a cursed bite out of him. I score two crits on a rat and Thomas finishes it with a firebolt, reducing it to ash. Baktha finishes the driver.

The vehicle is a **demon grinder**: speed 100, 200 hp, AC 19. One creature carried a **circlet of blasting**. I pull out the map and we roar off in our newly acquired demon grinder.

## Sundered Chains and Harroman's Hill
We cross a field of giant broken chains and a voice in my head says: "Sundered chains, as close to a place of pilgrimage as you'll find in this cursed place. A beacon to those seeking the missing link or looking for a way out." Three figures stand a bit ahead. We turn around and skirt wide, avoiding the devils working in the distance.

Later: Sticks Watchtower. "The Sticks is the demons' gateway into the nine hells. The devils keep a watchful eye on its shores." A huge alabaster tower just before the river Styx. We turn north with the river on our right. An hour later the grinder sputters. We feed it a soul coin.

Then Harroman's Hill. "Behold, Zariel's dog crucified those who betrayed his mistress in battle," the map announces. Wrought-iron trees line the hill. Anguished knights writhe in them while bloated stirges feast.

We follow the trees to a narrow land bridge over the river. The largest tree holds a crucified elf labeled [[Jander Sunstar]], who was [[Harroman]]'s right hand. "Please free me, please!" He's a vampire. Lulu says we can trust him, and Baktha's zone of truth backs her up.

Jander and Harroman were both part of the host that came through the portal. Jander retreated, closed the portal behind him, and threw himself at the feet of Lythander, who cursed him to an eternity of suffering in Avernus for his sins. We cut him down. As his feet touch ground, he crumbles to ash.

## Harroman
Thomas spots something coming from the northern sky: [[Harroman]], riding a nightmare. It destroys Baktha almost instantly. I run up and slash the nightmare's neck deep. Mike steps out and fires a lightning bolt. I kill the nightmare and Harroman knocks me unconscious.

When I come to, Harroman is dead on the ground in front of me. Baktha is still down, a swarm of stirges is closing in, and Lulu is gone, grappled by a hellwasp and carried west. We handle the stirges and lick our wounds. On the nightmare we find infernal tack, which looks very valuable.

## The Wasp Nest
West, a giant wasp nest floats in the sky, tethered to the land by iron chains. The map warns me off, lest my flesh become wasp food. Beneath the nest hangs a congealed mass of sticky organic matter. "I'm detecting something magical in there," says Baktha, who pulls off his boots and waddles through the goop, coming back with a **Storm Shortsword**.

We fail to climb the chains, so Mike misty steps into the nest and keeps us updated via Lulu. A few levels up he finds her stuck in the goo-coated walls. He gets briefly distracted discovering a **helm of telepathy** and pockets it quietly. Then he tries to free her with a plan that was not a good plan. He tugs on her a few times and attracts hell wasps. He thunderwaves them as they come to inspect, which drops his invisibility briefly, and the blast loosens Lulu enough for her to wriggle free. The wasps recover and slash at her, nearly killing her.

She heals herself and zips out the window toward the demon grinder. Mike, still invisible, dives out the window and lands light as a feather. The wasps spill out, annoyed, but don't notice them leaving. Meanwhile the rest of us haul the grinder behind some rubble to hide from the wasps.

Lulu arrives panting. The sword, she tells us, is either in the place where demons manifest, or the place where they are destroyed.
