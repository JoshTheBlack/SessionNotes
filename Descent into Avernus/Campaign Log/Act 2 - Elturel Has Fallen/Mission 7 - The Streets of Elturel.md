> [!abstract] The Job
> Cross the ruins of [[Elturel]] to [[High Hall]], past ghouls, zombies and devils, with the refugees in tow.

## The Tower in the Clouds
Reality has come unstitched. We ride griffins to a tower that floats, unmoored, high in the clouds. There we meet an odd otter and an elephant. Their names are [[Traxigor]] and [[Lulu]], and neither one looks like what it is.

[[Lulu]] speaks first, her memory a shattered mirror. She was with [[Zariel]] when the fabled Hellrider led the charge into [[Avernus]], and she helped [[Zariel]] hide her sword when some of the [[Hellriders]] turned on her. The rest of it is fog.

Without ceremony, [[Traxigor]] casts *planeshift*. The serene clouds are ripped away and we land in the acrid, burning air of [[Elturel]]. A city of death. Black smoke sears our noses and scrapes our lungs. [[Traxigor]] vanishes. [[Lulu]], in a flash of clarity, remembers she can turn into a mammoth and use some kind of magic.

Elturel is a hellscape. The streets run with magma. Giant black chains rise out of the ground, holding the whole city suspended over a pit of infernal horror. We follow [[Lulu]] toward the keep.

## A City in Torment
First we find a woman, [[Harkina Hunt]], and her two toddlers, running from a pack of fiends. We charge in and burn them to ash with divine fire. [[Harkina Hunt|Harkina]] tells us of the countless dead. No food, no water, and creatures preying on anyone who goes looking. She's headed for [[High Hall]], and we offer to escort her.

On the way she tells us about the constant war between devils and demons. A block on, goat devils are tearing into a man, surrounded by an aura of filth. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] starts coughing up flies but kills one. Boils erupt on my skin just before I kill the other. The man is [[Oren Ragron]], a blacksmith, who's been hiding and foraging, unable to get back to his shop.

A building collapses and cries rise from the rubble. We dig. [[Lulu]]'s uncanny communication leads us to three trapped dwarves: [[Valkora Ashenwell]], a mason and acolyte of [[Moraden]], along with [[Stroven Ironfist]] and [[Cartra Boldenstern]]. [[Valkora Ashenwell|Valkora]] is also on her way to High Hall, to help with the rebuilding.

When we try to scout the bridge, ghouls swarm us. [[Thomus - Thomas]] throws a distraction flare to protect the dwarves, and they turn on us, clawing and biting. I bash one in. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] steps between two of them and drops a lightning bolt that hits seven of the eight, killing five outright. We finish the last.

One ghoul wore a nice set of studded leather. I needed better protection. It turns out to be **+1 studded leather**. A tall building nearby gives us a view of the bridge and the heavy guard on it. We rest for the night. [[Salah Secumber - Hal]] digs through a pile of filth and comes up with a **ring of invisibility**. Of course he does.

## A Dark Bargain
In the morning we head north. An imp is menacing a halfling, and I put an arrow through its throat. [[Reya Mantlemorn]] rushes up and finishes the job. The halfling is [[Pillster Pebblechuck]], a baker before the descent. He and his family had run out of food, and the imp, [[Perchulux]], offered a month of food and water in exchange for his soul. He took the deal. We arrived just in time.

We read the contract. A hidden clause gives the imp the souls of his whole family. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] incinerates it with sacred flame, and half the imp's corpse vanishes with it, leaving behind a mysterious iron disc, five inches wide and an inch thick, that feels like it holds something inside. [[Lulu]] knows it: a **soul coin**, the currency of Avernus, with a trapped soul inside.

[[Lulu]]'s memory returns in a flash: **[[Yeenoghu]]**, a demon lord, wants to destroy [[Zariel]]'s sword, a goal he once fought for. She can't remember where, but it's locked in now.

We detour to fetch [[Pillster Pebblechuck|Pillster]]'s starving family and feed them rations. They join our little band of refugees and heroes. It's a caravan now. I'm mostly a cook.

## The North Bridge
As we approach the north bridge, a rotten stench hits us, a hundred times worse than any corpse. Through a window I see four shadowy figures gnashing their teeth and devouring something. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] throws a fireball in. I charge, the stench clawing at my throat and blurring my vision. They swarm me, biting and clawing. My muscles seize, nearly paralyzed, but I shake it off. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] hits them with a bolt of frost, but [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]]'s holy flame sputters out before reaching them.

[[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] and [[Reya Mantlemorn|Reya]] move in, seemingly immune to the smell. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] and [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] burn another with spells and [[Lulu]] takes out the last of the **ghasts**. Hiding in a cupboard, crying, [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] finds a small girl, [[Shora Hevron]], who clings to him and asks if her family is gone. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] heals me.

The tower nearest the bridge is an old schoolhouse, now packed with zombies. We hoist [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] to a second-story window and he rains down a fireball. I try to kick the door open but the zombies pile against it. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] follows with burning hands, killing half of the dozen. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] bursts in and takes five with thunderwave. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] finishes the last.

In the corner, a floor hatch leads to a stash of fifteen gallons of water in flasks. I also find a crate with a false bottom holding prized possessions and a letter signed by a "Fraxus" who hid his belongings there.

During a short rest, [[Lulu]] remembers hiding in the **[[The Wandering Emporium|Wandering Emporium]]**, and thinks it might be able to help us.

We set a trap for the bridge. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]], wearing his new ring of invisibility, goes ahead to prepare a thunderwave. I hobble along like a feeble old man with the rest in tow. Two bearded devils and four spined devils arrive. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] spots a rune on the arch and activates it, and every devil on the bridge takes radiant damage. They scream. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]]'s thunderwave kills four. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] kills a fifth. I put an arrow through the last one's brain. We cross the bridge mostly unmolested.

## The Road to High Hall
On the far side, a burned human corpse. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] reaches for a silvered sword in its hand and triggers a trap of hellfire. He puts himself out, then collects the **silvered sword** and a **potion of frost giant strength**.

Further on, a vulture-like devil perched in a burned tree reaches into our minds. This is [[Abathrax]]. He doesn't seem hostile, just contemplative and worn out by the entire descent. As we pass, he says only: "Mind the portal."

Ahead, an armored creature with a battleaxe steps out of an alley, followed by four spined devils. He looks exasperated, like a teacher herding unruly children. A sudden quake knocks [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] and [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] down, and two of the spined devils too. They shout, alerting the rest. I rush the first and [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] finishes it with a radiant bolt. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] and [[Reya Mantlemorn|Reya]] kill a second. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] gets up and casts guiding bolt, [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] knocks another back with thunderwave. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] and I focus on the big one and drop him in a single round. We finish the last two.

[[Lulu]] warns us of a **Narzugon** riding a nightmare down a nearby street. We duck into a building and hold our breath until it passes. After a short rest we go on, using [[Lulu]]'s eyes in the sky to reach the base of [[High Hall]] undetected.
