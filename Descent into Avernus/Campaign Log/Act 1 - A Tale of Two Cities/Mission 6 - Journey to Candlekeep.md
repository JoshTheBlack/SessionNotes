> [!abstract] The Job
> Get out of [[Baldur's Gate]], get to [[Candlekeep]], and find out what [[Thavius Kreeg]]'s puzzle box is hiding. Then find a way into [[Elturel]].

## Getting Out of Town
The stink of [[Vanthampur Villa]] is still in my nose when we report the whole mess to [[Darmin Zodge (Captain)]]. We're interrupted halfway through by the arrival of [[Commander Liara Portyr]] at the Black Gate. A veteran of Fort Beluarian in Chult, summoned home by her uncle to run [[The Flaming Fist]] while [[Ulder Ravengard]] is missing. A brass hat. I put my hand on my wallet by reflex.

We tell her we need to leave the city. Our hunch: [[Thavius Kreeg]] had a hand in the fall of [[Elturel]]. Porter, to my surprise, is reasonable. She gives us horses, provisions, and a squad of guards to escort us to the northwest gate. The price: she confiscates our Flaming Fist badges. Fine by me. I never liked wearing a leash.

## A Bookworm Joins the Party
Next stop: [[Falaster Fisk]]. When he hears we have the puzzle box, he nearly bursts. He packs in a hurry, wanting to come along to [[Candlekeep]], and he's brought a book called *[[Tastes of Callamshite]]*. I hope it's a cookbook. It is not.

We cross the great bridges to the tall rocky island in the middle of the Chionthar. They're so clogged with shops and stalls that you can't even see the river. A narrow channel of road wiggles through the middle. Drawbridges lead to a stout keep on the island flying the flags of the city and the Fist. I'd love to burn the lot.

## Highway Robbery (Theirs)
We catch a couple of pickpockets. One of them I send on his way with a ration and a suggestion to take the day off. A hungry thief is just a man who needs lunch.

Further on, a wagon driven by an ordinary farmer. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] spots the glint of a weapon moving in the hay and, without a word, sets the hay on fire. The farmer is a *fiend* in disguise, red wings and all. I ride alongside and sink my shortsword in. It doesn't care. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]]'s arrow misses. [[Salah Secumber - Hal]]'s magical mace is what bothers it, and it takes to the sky, sixty feet up, hurling firebolts back at us.

A long, patient fight, but we put it and its buddies in the dirt. On the fiend's corpse, a magical spear: a **flashfire spear**. Spoils.

## The Road
That first night we camp under stars so thick they look spilled. [[Reya Mantlemorn]] tells us a tale of her people.

West of [[Elturel]], fiends used to steal women and children. In desperation, the High Rider told his people to pray for divine help. Their prayers were answered: the goddess [[Zariel]] herself appeared, found the fiendish portal and led the Highriders through to [[Avernus]]. The portal buckled and collapsed under the weight of so many brave souls. Thus the [[Hellriders]] were born.

Day two: nothing. I almost enjoyed it.

Day three: smoke on the horizon. Two burning wagons and two ogres smashing around them. We charge. I hit one with my shortsword. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] and [[Thomus - Thomas]] each fire a firebolt. [[Reya Mantlemorn|Reya]]'s hammer cracks bone. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]]'s guiding bolt shines on the ogre's hide. I land a crit. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]]'s firebolt misses its target but singes close enough to finish the beast. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] takes the second ogre and I deliver the last blow.

We find strange tribal armbands on the bodies and take them, hoping [[Sylvira Savikas]] knows what they mean. A few survivors tell us they're refugees from Elturel, turned away at the gates of Baldur's Gate, harassed on the road, then ambushed by the ogres. Another reason I have no use for gates.

Day four: rain. Heavy, steady and miserable, just like the refugees.

Day five: a crossroads. One sign says "The Way of the Lion." The other, "Candlekeep." [[Falaster Fisk|Fisk]] assures us that The Way of the Lion is the fastest way to Candlekeep, which makes no sense whatsoever, but he's the scholar, so we follow.

## Candlekeep
At the gate, three monks greet us: a human, an elf and a dwarf. The elf demands a gift for entry. [[Falaster Fisk|Fisk]] grins and hands over *[[Tastes of Callamshite]]*. They accept it with surprising grace, and we're in.

[[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] shows the monks the ogre armbands. They recognize the markings: an infernal cult. We find the inn where, in the corner, sits a lone ogre reading *The Sum of Theology* by Saint Visera. He's wearing an ornate headband. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]], in a fit of audacious curiosity, walks over and asks him about the armbands. He doesn't recognize them but finds them tribal and charming, then tells a tale about his headband: taken from a halfling he slew in his brutish days. Ever since wearing it, he's been a hungry student of knowledge and goes by [[Little One]] in the halfling's honor. Candlekeep. Where even the ogres read.

## Sylvira Savikas
The next morning we find [[Sylvira Savikas]] in a chamber of the keep, in wizard's robes, standing on a huge nine-pointed star on the floor with a spiny quasit at her side: her familiar, Jezebel. "I can't tell you how pleased I am to meet you. What have you brought me?"

I hand over the puzzle box. She studies it a good long while and tells us she's long suspected [[Thavius Kreeg|Kreeg]] conspired with devils in the fall of Elturel, and that the proof is in the box. Jezebel fetches a flask of liquid, which [[Sylvira Savikas|Sylvira]] pours over the box. It crawls over the surface in defiance of gravity like a halo, then, with a soft *click*, the box falls open.

Inside: nine chain-linked plates, three inches a side, stamped with infernal runes. [[Reya Mantlemorn|Reya]] reads them aloud.

> Be it known to all that I, **[[Thavius Kreeg]]**, High Overseer of Elturel, have sworn to my master, **[[Zariel]]**, lord of Avernus, to keep the agreements contained in this oath.
>
> I hereby submit to [[Zariel]] in all matters and for all time. I will place Her above all creatures, living and dead. I will obey Her all my days and beyond with fear and servility.
>
> I recognize the dispensation of the device called the [[The Companion|Solar Insidiator]], hereafter called the [[The Companion|Companion]]. In my capacity as High Overseer of Elturel and its vassal territories, I acknowledge that all lands falling under the light of the Companion are forfeit to [[Zariel]]. All persons bound by oath to defend Elturel are also considered forfeit. I further recognize that this dispensation will last fifty years, after which the Companion will return whence it came, taking Elturel and its oath-bound defenders with it, if that is [[Zariel]]'s wish.
>
> All this is my everlasting pledge.

Halfway through, [[Reya Mantlemorn|Reya]] starts to weep. When she took the oath of the Hell Knights, she pledged her soul to [[Zariel]] without knowing it. I don't have words for that. For once.

## The Shield
We ask [[Sylvira Savikas|Sylvira]] about the [[Shield of the Hidden Lord]]. It's a symbol of good triumphing over evil, she says, but the thing inside, [[Gargauth]], is a pit fiend sent by Asmodeus to corrupt mortals, and has grown into a sort of demigod. The shield corrupts everything near it. The Hhune family has guarded it for centuries and should *not* get it back. Better to keep it in an extradimensional space, or carry it into the fiery pits of [[Avernus]].

As she speaks, [[Gargauth]]'s voice rolls through my mind, begging me to do exactly that, promising to serve as my guide and counselor. I reply with a mental image of me slapping it silly and holding a finger to my lips: *shush*.

[[Sylvira Savikas|Sylvira]] tells us a dragon named [[Traxigor]] can get us to Elturel, where we can make our descent. We should also speak to a creature named [[Lulu]] at [[Traxigor]]'s tower. She leads us to some saddled griffins and sends us flying.

With a final word of warning, she leads us to a row of saddled griffins. Up we go, toward [[Traxigor]]'s tower. At last, a clear purpose. I don't trust it one bit.
