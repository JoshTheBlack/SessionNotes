> [!abstract] The Job
> Clear the [[Dead Three]] out of [[The Bathhouse]] and whatever is festering beneath it.

## The Plan
Recon first. We filter into the bathhouse one at a time over half an hour or so, like honest customers. Me, I'd have kicked the door in, but the wizard wanted a plan, and apparently plans are a thing now.

On the walk over, every second tongue in the street is wagging about [[Elturel]]. Nobody knows anything. Everybody's sure.

## The Recon
[[The Bathhouse]] is a big stucco building with a clay-tile roof and a courtyard. Fancy, in the way rotten fruit is fancy.

[[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] goes in first, [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] right behind. [[Thomus - Thomas]] books a massage, and an attendant named Qurmilah tells him the warm water comes from an elaborate system of pipes under the building. He also spots a false wall in the massage room. [[Salah Secumber - Hal]] gets rubbed down by a fellow named Jabaz in the other chamber. Half a dozen locals are splashing around the shallow pools, and not a single attendant in sight.

I stay outside. Someone has to be the lookout, and I'm very, very good at being the lookout.

## The Docks
We leave with a plan to come back after dark and take a stroll down to the docks to look over the *[[Uncivil Serpent]]*. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] floats the idea of having his father outfit her and sail under his family's banners, in exchange for donating the ship when we're done.

A ship. Of our own. I can taste the salt pork already.

## The Infiltration
Night falls. We creep back to a window at the rear of the bathhouse. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] gets a boost, pries it open and tosses down a rope. Then three figures in crimson robes drop out of the shadows onto him.

Blades flash. Three wounds. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] answers with a *boom*, a thunderwave that hurls one cultist through the doorway and into the bathwater and slams the other two against the stone.

I haul myself up the rope. "Are they dead?" I ask, and in my enthusiasm, accidentally smack one of [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]]'s wounds. He took it well. Eventually.

One of the cultists is trying to stand. My blade makes the decision for him. Two more come through the door. One buries a dagger in [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]]'s chest. The other's eyes glow green and he fires a ray of black necromantic muck that just misses me. I'd like to say I wasn't scared. I'd like to say a lot of things.

[[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] and [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] pile in, and [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] launches himself off the window ledge onto one of them. I charge the necromancer to throw him in the bath, but he's planted like an iron post. So I draw my shortsword and open his throat instead. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] finishes the straggler. We strip the bodies and go down.

## The Dungeon
Knee-deep, freezing water. Damp and rot in the air. A hidden passage opens. In the first chamber, a bloated corpse floats in the dark, two days dead. Nice touch.

East of that sits an altar slick with blood, topped with a human skull frozen in a scream. South, an alcove with a tapestry showing four faceless figures dismembering a fifth. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] touches it. Of course he does. It bursts in a cloud of yellow spores and poisons him half to death.

The path rises out of the water into an octagonal chamber with three doors, each bearing a god's face: [[Bhaal]] to the north, [[Bane]] to the east, [[Myrkul]] to the south.

[[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] opens the north door, which loops back to the entrance. Drawn to [[Bane]]'s black hand, I take the east, a long hallway of short stairs ending in a flooded chamber. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] takes the south.

Inside: three robed figures sleeping in a triangle around a single torch. One wakes and glares at [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]]. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] charges in and crushes a skull before it can rise. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] lights up another with a firebolt, and [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]], in his enthusiasm, singes off his own eyebrows. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] finishes the last with a firebolt.

Past the sleepers, a looted sarcophagus. Three spellbooks inside: a crimson one of fire and illusion, a brown one with a personal rune full of utility enchantments, and one bound in black scales, all shadow and darkness.

On the eastern path, up a short flight of stairs, we find a battered elf chained to the wall and two tormenters having fun with him: a woman in chainmail with a wooden shield, and a brute in a plate helm and chain shirt. We hit them hard. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]]'s guiding bolt goes wide, [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]]'s firebolt glances. The woman, [[The Fist of Bane]], swings at [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] and misses. My first stab glances, the second misses. The brute, [[The Iron Consult]], bellows and provokes her into attacking again.

[[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] drops the [[The Fist of Bane|Fist of Bane]]. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] finishes the [[The Iron Consult|Iron Consult]]. The elf is free of his misery, in a manner of speaking.

A suit of armor with arcane writing on its gauntlets. A burlap sack behind its legs. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] touches it. The gauntlets *detach* and go to work on him. We smash them, but not before they knock [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] senseless. Lesson learned: never touch anything. Except food.

Back in the room behind [[Myrkul]]'s door, we rest. Footsteps. Voices. We set an ambush. Three acolytes in red with skull flails step in and get the surprise of their lives. Two drop. The third bolts and we chase him to a hidden chamber where a necromancer is mid-ritual, swirling with rats. She drops a fireball on most of [[==Umbra Contundens==]] and we cut her down anyway. A padlock and key are tangled in her hair. Turn the key, the lock opens, and a spectral skull rises and stares into each of us with silent laughter. I was off guarding the passage. Smart is the new lucky.

Next, an altar of skulls and bones. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] ignites a brazier, then all of them flare to life, burning green. An inscription: "Rise and be counted." [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] reads it aloud, and the bones on the altar rise into three skeletons that obey *him*. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Hal]] the necromancer. Fantastic.

The next flooded room reeks of methane and sulfur. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]], in his infinite wisdom, throws a firebolt in. The room goes off like a keg. He and [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] are knocked flat, [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] is badly burned. *Brilliant.*

Down a long hallway of side corridors, some collapsed. In one, [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] finds a stone vault with a skeleton on it, a false bottom, and under it a mummy floating in briny red liquid with moonstones for eyes and a *bag of beans* stuffed down its throat.

[[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] sends a skeleton to unbar another door, and six zombies lunge out. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] flees into a room smeared with blood, two flayed bodies shackled to the wall, a cat-o'-nine-tails and a bucket of salt on the floor. I cleave a zombie in two and we form a killbox in the hallway. They come at us one by one and meet bludgeoning, slicing and searing.

In the torture chamber, [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] finds a dying Tiefling, [[Vendetta Kress]], and heals her for 8 hit points. She distributes wine for the [[Oathoon Family]]'s establishments, was tortured senseless, and can tell us nothing. We rest in a cleared room, then walk her back to the street. Then back down.

## The Lower Depths
Past the torture chamber, stairs descend to a fork: one door south, two east. Behind the second eastern door, four armored men are asleep. I slip in and open one throat. Then the next, and my armor clanks. The other three wake. I gut a second and we finish the rest.

In the last room, a sarcophagus brimming with blood. A frail old woman steps out from behind it. "Thank goodness, you're here! My heroes!" [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]], ever cautious, casts detect evil, and her glamour drops. A hag. She slashes [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] to ribbons and vanishes.

I swing blind at where she should be. She slides a charm into my head, and for a moment I'm her loyal protector, my blade turned on [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]]. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]]'s thunderwave shatters the spell and kills her. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] and I both pay for it. We rest.

During [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]]'s watch a monstrous commotion thunders by: an ogre-like brute dragging a giant bat. We follow behind. A hidden door, a flooded hallway. In the east room, four bone-white skeletons rise from the water and mob [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]]. I smash one, [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]]'s thunderwave takes three more, [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] finishes the last.

At the end, atop a heap of corpses, a scarred giant with a club fights a shirtless man with a skull for a head and two scimitars. The giant is [[Mortlock Vanthampur]]. The skull-faced one flees.

[[Mortlock Vanthampur|Mortlock]] talks. His own family is paying the [[Dead Three]] to murder citizens, to weaken [[The Flaming Fist]] and open a path for [[Thalamra Vanthampur]] to take [[The Council of Four]] before [[Baldur's Gate]] falls to infernal forces. His brothers hired the cultists to kill *him*. [[Amrik Vanthampur]], proprietor of [[The Low Lantern]], pays the cultists regularly. [[Thurstwell Vanthampur]], too sickly to leave his mother's estate, uses imps as spies around the city and has almost certainly been watching us.

We chase the skull-headed leader into a hall of colossal wooden statues of the Dead Three. He's good. He drops [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]]. [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]] lands a guiding bolt and we finish him in a nasty exchange. We pick the compound clean for a modest haul of coin, gems and jewelry.

Out on the street we meet Tiamat's cultists. Their leader, Ultiss, proclaims our doom. We cut through them like soup. Then it's back to the *[[Uncivil Serpent]]* to sleep.

## Loose Ends
We trade loot for coin with every merchant who'll deal, then report to [[Darmin Zodge (Captain)]]. He's grim but satisfied and gives us our next job: kill [[Amrik Vanthampur]]. The Vanthampur web runs deep, and someone has to start cutting threads.
