> [!abstract] The Job
> Cross the ruins of [[Elturel]], find [[Ulder Ravenguard]], and find a way down into [[Avernus]].

## The Tower in the Clouds
Reality has come unstitched. We ride griffins to a tower that floats, unmoored, high in the clouds. There we meet an odd otter and an elephant. Their names are [[Traxicore]] and [[Lulu]], and neither one looks like what it is.

Lulu speaks first, her memory a shattered mirror. She was with [[Zariel]] when the fabled Hellrider led the charge into Avernus, and she helped Zariel hide her sword when some of the Hellriders turned on her. The rest of it is fog.

Without ceremony, Traxicore casts *planeshift*. The serene clouds are ripped away and we land in the acrid, burning air of [[Elturel]]. A city of death. Black smoke sears our noses and scrapes our lungs. Traxicore vanishes. Lulu, in a flash of clarity, remembers she can turn into a mammoth and use some kind of magic.

Elturel is a hellscape. The streets run with magma. Giant black chains rise out of the ground, holding the whole city suspended over a pit of infernal horror. We follow Lulu toward the keep.

## A City in Torment
First we find a woman, [[Harkina Hunt]], and her two toddlers, running from a pack of fiends. We charge in and burn them to ash with divine fire. Harkina tells us of the countless dead. No food, no water, and creatures preying on anyone who goes looking. She's headed for [[High Hall]], and we offer to escort her.

On the way she tells us about the constant war between devils and demons. A block on, goat devils are tearing into a man, surrounded by an aura of filth. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] starts coughing up flies but kills one. Boils erupt on my skin just before I kill the other. The man is [[Oren Ragron]], a blacksmith, who's been hiding and foraging, unable to get back to his shop.

A building collapses and cries rise from the rubble. We dig. Lulu's uncanny communication leads us to three trapped dwarves: [[Valkora Ashenwell]], a mason and acolyte of [[Moraden]], along with [[Stroven Ironfist]] and [[Cartra Boldenstern]]. Valkora is also on her way to High Hall, to help with the rebuilding.

When we try to scout the bridge, ghouls swarm us. [[Thomus - Thomas]] throws a distraction flare to protect the dwarves, and they turn on us, clawing and biting. I bash one in. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] steps between two of them and drops a lightning bolt that hits seven of the eight, killing five outright. We finish the last.

One ghoul wore a nice set of studded leather. I needed better protection. It turns out to be **+1 studded leather**. A tall building nearby gives us a view of the bridge and the heavy guard on it. We rest for the night. [[Salah Secumber - Hal]] digs through a pile of filth and comes up with a **ring of invisibility**. Of course he does.

## A Dark Bargain
In the morning we head north. An imp is menacing a halfling, and I put an arrow through its throat. [[Reya Mantlemorn]] rushes up and finishes the job. The halfling is [[Pillster Pebblechuck]], a baker before the descent. He and his family had run out of food, and the imp, [[Perchulux]], offered a month of food and water in exchange for his soul. He took the deal. We arrived just in time.

We read the contract. A hidden clause gives the imp the souls of his whole family. Baktha incinerates it with sacred flame, and half the imp's corpse vanishes with it, leaving behind a mysterious iron disc, five inches wide and an inch thick, that feels like it holds something inside. Lulu knows it: a **soul coin**, the currency of Avernus, with a trapped soul inside.

Lulu's memory returns in a flash: **[[Yenego]]**, a demon lord, wants to destroy Zariel's sword, a goal he once fought for. She can't remember where, but it's locked in now.

We detour to fetch Pillster's starving family and feed them rations. They join our little band of refugees and heroes. It's a caravan now. I'm mostly a cook.

## The North Bridge
As we approach the north bridge, a rotten stench hits us, a hundred times worse than any corpse. Through a window I see four shadowy figures gnashing their teeth and devouring something. Thomas throws a fireball in. I charge, the stench clawing at my throat and blurring my vision. They swarm me, biting and clawing. My muscles seize, nearly paralyzed, but I shake it off. Mike hits them with a bolt of frost, but Salah's holy flame sputters out before reaching them.

Baktha and Reya move in, seemingly immune to the smell. Salah and Thomas burn another with spells and Lulu takes out the last of the **ghasts**. Hiding in a cupboard, crying, Baktha finds a small girl, [[Shora Hevron]], who clings to him and asks if her family is gone. Salah heals me.

The tower nearest the bridge is an old schoolhouse, now packed with zombies. We hoist Thomas to a second-story window and he rains down a fireball. I try to kick the door open but the zombies pile against it. Thomas follows with burning hands, killing half of the dozen. Mike bursts in and takes five with thunderwave. Baktha finishes the last.

In the corner, a floor hatch leads to a stash of fifteen gallons of water in flasks. I also find a crate with a false bottom holding prized possessions and a letter signed by a "Fraxus" who hid his belongings there.

During a short rest, Lulu remembers hiding in the **Wandering Emporium**, and thinks it might be able to help us.

We set a trap for the bridge. Mike, wearing his new ring of invisibility, goes ahead to prepare a thunderwave. I hobble along like a feeble old man with the rest in tow. Two bearded devils and four spined devils arrive. Baktha spots a rune on the arch and activates it, and every devil on the bridge takes radiant damage. They scream. Mike's thunderwave kills four. Baktha kills a fifth. I put an arrow through the last one's brain. We cross the bridge mostly unmolested.

## The Road to High Hall
On the far side, a burned human corpse. Baktha reaches for a silvered sword in its hand and triggers a trap of hellfire. He puts himself out, then collects the **silvered sword** and a **potion of frost giant strength**.

Further on, a vulture-like devil perched in a burned tree reaches into our minds. This is [[Abathrax]]. He doesn't seem hostile, just contemplative and worn out by the entire descent. As we pass, he says only: "Mind the portal."

Ahead, an armored creature with a battleaxe steps out of an alley, followed by four spined devils. He looks exasperated, like a teacher herding unruly children. A sudden quake knocks Salah and Mike down, and two of the spined devils too. They shout, alerting the rest. I rush the first and Thomas finishes it with a radiant bolt. Baktha and Reya kill a second. Salah gets up and casts guiding bolt, Mike knocks another back with thunderwave. Thomas and I focus on the big one and drop him in a single round. We finish the last two.

Lulu warns us of a **Narzugon** riding a nightmare down a nearby street. We duck into a building and hold our breath until it passes. After a short rest we go on, using Lulu's eyes in the sky to reach the base of [[High Hall]] undetected.

## High Hall
The keep is partly destroyed and heavily charred. Two hellhounds guard the gate. I charge, but before I can reach them, Mike's lightning bolt and Salah's guiding bolt drop both. The entrance is a column-lined hallway, some columns carved in honor of [[Torm]], others defaced with Zariel's sigil. Beyond a closed door is a foyer.

Behind a curtain, an altar with a lever beside it. A big devil patrols the altar, flanked by six black-robed humans. They spot us, and I yelp a warning.

Thomas sweeps the curtain aside and drops a fireball that turns the cultists to ash and doesn't faze the devil. I rush in, slash and bash. Mike hits it with a ray of frost; nothing. The devil unleashes a flurry on me and I dodge every one. After a few rounds of stalemate, Lulu flies in and blasts it with a *trumpet of sparkles*, melting it into confetti. I've seen a lot of things die. Confetti is new.

In an alcove north of the altar we find [[Seltern Obranch]], a druid and the last of the temple's defenders to fall in this area. A large force of devils assaulted the cathedral. Everyone left alive fled to the crypts. He's been keeping his friends alive with *goodberry*. (Finally, someone who understands the importance of a snack.)

Upstairs, a massive pipe organ. Cowering in the northern tower, a guard named [[Trevick Fanthorm]], whom we coax out. Before heading back down, Salah sits at the organ and plays a little ditty that fills us all with inspiration. From the railing we watch a patrol of eight giant crabs and a devil go by, and wait until they're gone.

At the rear of the temple, another curtain hides a desecrated private altar, another devil, and eight more giant crabs. They rush me. I crit the devil and step back out of reach. Thomas lifts the curtain and throws a fireball, killing every crab and doing nothing to the devil. The devil clips Baktha. I step up and crit again. Salah lands a guiding bolt, and Baktha finishes him.

## The Catacombs
Two towers behind the altar are destroyed. We head down into the catacombs. In an ossuary to the west, a few survivors are hiding. Salah notices a sarcophagus that's been tampered with and pries it open. A mummy pours out. I whack it, Salah blasts it, it whacks me. Another round and Salah finishes it. It wears **bracers of defense +2**, which Mike claims, ditching his studded leather, and a golden symbol of [[Baphomet]] around its neck. That's a curious thing to find in a Torm temple.

Up the north hallway is a hall of scholars with a potion and some red amethysts. Near the entrance, Baktha notices a greatsword resting in an ossuary. It looks like a sword of legend, but strangely not magical.

We meet [[Phyria Jynks]], timid until she sees Baktha and Salah wearing the symbols of Torm. She carries the tome of the [[Creed Resolute]], a codex listing every citizen of Elturel who has taken the oath to [[Elturgard]]. She's reluctant to share more, unsure whether she can protect it. All the names across the ages are in the same handwriting, so someone has been guarding this for ages.

We use a wand at a wall and discover a secret door, and the same wand reveals the greatsword as a **[[Holy Avenger]]**. Salah dips it in a fountain of holy water and says a prayer of reconsecration, and hands it to Baktha. Baktha, delighted with his new toy, hands me his silvered longsword. I hand the mace back to Salah.

The secret door leads to a tunnel to a cemetery on the west side of Elturel. Phyria says [[Ulder Ravenguard]] left through this tunnel days ago and should be back by now. We take a long rest in the catacombs. Reya decides to stay with her people, as is her charge. We leave a scroll of thunderwave to help protect the refugees, and, after a prayer to Torm, we part ways with her. A quiet, earnest goodbye. I'm not saying it got to me.

## The Cemetery
The tunnel is mostly uneventful until it opens to the outside. We send Lulu ahead invisibly and she finds an extremely steep, jagged drop, less steep going up. It looks like a giant took a bite out of Elturel's west side. Phyria's information was a little out of date.

We climb up. A massive cemetery, graves dug up from the inside or the outside, who knows. A chapel pulses with fetid purple radiance. We start down the path when I hear galloping behind us. Four skeletal minotaurs charge. I fire first with my longbow from the back, Thomas drops a fireball, Baktha meets the nearest with his Holy Avenger and it's the worst day of that minotaur's afterlife. Mike notices they're lined up and fries them with a lightning bolt. Salah throws a guiding bolt. I hit with my newly acquired silvered longsword like I've owned it for years. Baktha obliterates one with a smite, then falls to the next. Thomas stabilizes him with *spare the dying*. I crush the last. We take their greataxes, all four.

We lick our wounds and make a beeline for the chapel, stained glass shattered everywhere. Pillars in front list the names of heroes: Agnithar, Zokel of Torm, Bertra Zornes, Yevina Druen, Ca'sar, Xivik Nue, VanLancer Eagletalon. I whisper each as I pass and the pillars light up. A swarm of shadows pours out. We deal with them in thirty seconds. Salah turns undead and three flee. Baktha kills two with the Holy Avenger, Mike one, me one.

Thomas walks in the front door and four more minotaur skeletons meet him. It takes about ten rounds, but we grind them to dust. Then Thomas says a short prayer and pulls a **+2 longbow of Lathander** out of thin air. Baktha does the same and pulls out a **+2 longsword of Lathander**, which he hands to me. I don't ask questions of free weapons.

## Gideon and the Grand Duke
Salah peeks through a curtain into another room and is confronted by a gaunt man with long nails and dreadlocks, flanked by two insectoids. "Greetings," he rasps. Friendly, if gruff. We barely start talking when a mass of dretches pours up the stairs and attacks him and his familiars, followed by a giant scorpion.

Thomas fires a guiding bolt at the scorpion. I close in. Baktha vaporizes a dretch with the Holy Avenger. Mike misses. The gruff man kills one, I kill another, Baktha kills the last, leaving only the scorpion. It cripples Baktha. The gruff man swings his fist wildly at it and then falls into a trance, and the scorpion drops dead from a withering gaze.

His name is [[Gideon]]. He serves some unnamed devil master and is posted here as a sentinel against demons.

We go down into the dungeon below, following human tracks through the debris, until we reach a small wading pool where a man lies in the water, screaming, trying to pull a helmet off his head. Four skeletal minotaurs attack and we deal with them quickly, but not before they knock me unconscious. Salah heals me and we finish them.

Then we realize the screaming man is [[Ulder Ravenguard]], and three minotaurs stream out of a portal. They swerve around him at the last second and attack us, knocking me and Baktha down hard. Salah patches us up, and not a moment too soon, because another volley knocks Baktha out and hurts me badly. Salah brings Baktha back from the brink and we grind the beasts to dust.

We can't get the helm off Ulder. Salah tries *bless* and in a flash of clarity Ulder mutters a name: "Feria." Then he's back to babbling. Mike goes invisible and lifts Gideon's Testament from his chambers, because of course he does, and we go back for Feria.

We stop in the tunnel short of our gaggle of survivors and Salah goes in alone to get her. [[Phyria Jynks]], also called Feria, tells us to take Ulder to the altar of Torm. Baktha touches the Holy Avenger to the helm, channeling celestial light, and two orbs of unholy energy pop out of its horns. I move in and attack one. We take turns until they dissipate. After a few more minutes of prayer, Ulder sits up, clear-eyed, and takes off the helm, grateful and free.

## What the Elephant Remembers
Lulu starts talking about how she used to shapechange. Something about being Zariel's mount, shoving a sword in a rock. A demon that wants to destroy it and hates Zariel. She wandered Avernus and hid in the Wandering Emporium, whose proprietor might help us. Zariel is gone. Her last words were something about demons crying out her name when they die. Lulu thinks there may still be goodness in her.

Feria asks Ulder what happened with that helm. He guesses the evil from the portal was absorbed into the helmet and he got caught inside. We ask what he knows about the fall of Elturel. Not much, so we tell him what we found about [[Thavius Kreeg]], the contract, and the Vanthampurs trying to do the same to Baldur's Gate.

He asks Lulu to help him remember what he saw under the helm's control. She dives into his mind, and he describes it.

> [!quote]- Ulder's Vision
> "I saw a bloodied woman in armor, a soldier wearing the colors and crest of Elturel, grasping a longsword fit for an angel. Fresh blood streamed from a nasty cut on the woman's cheek.
>
> "Flying next to the woman was a small, golden elephant with rapidly fluttering wings.
>
> "As an enormous, loping demon threatened to devour the woman, she plunged the sword into the ground while the winged elephant made a trumpet sound with its trunk. The demon was hurled back as an alabaster palace rose up around the sword. The winged elephant fled and took to the red sky of Avernus, where it watched as a bloody scab grew up from the ground to engulf the palace and the enormous demon.
>
> "The winged elephant fled and wandered in a delirium before coming upon two odd, birdlike humanoids dressed in patchwork armor and standing next to a strange infernal vehicle. The bird creatures were equipped with odd weapons and tools."

Lulu says she's still missing some memories. She takes the helm from Ulder and puts it on. It conforms to her head and she flies up, refreshed. The two bird creatures, [[Chucka]] and [[Clonk]], helped her. They took her to a junkyard called [[Fort Knucklebone]] and helped her build an infernal vehicle to travel the wastes of Avernus.

We rest, then scrounge for magic items to help us make the rest of the descent. After some earnest searching, we come up with six **potions of flying** and a **magical shortsword of thunder**.
