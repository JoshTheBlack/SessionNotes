> [!abstract] The Job
> Get into [[The Bleeding Citadel]].

## The Scab
With our freshly marked map leading the way, we head for the Bleeding Citadel. A few hours later we arrive. A great, disgusting scab the size of a hill rises from a stinking swamp of blood. The domed top of an alabaster temple pokes up through it. Black iron chains converge on it from every direction.

We try to scale the chains. [[Thomus - Thomas]] and I make it. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]], [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] and [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah]] fall into the clotted moat and have to swim out. We help them up. Inside is a cavernous system of fleshy tunnels.

On one side, the scab has been stripped from the passage, revealing a stained glass window of a contemplative angel in opaque blues and yellows. We can't open it, so we go on, up the rope and down the fleshy tunnel.

## The Tunnels
A junction leads down to a room with five goat-headed demons around a pool of blood. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] hits two with a lightning bolt and I rush in screaming. Four swings and one lies at my feet. Four more and another. I kick them into the blood pool. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] throws some lightning, and [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah|Zaleria]] crits one with a guiding bolt and steals one of my kills. We finish the rest. In the pool floats a corpse with a flask of water from the river Styx and a diamond worth 5,000 gp.

Further on is a 40-foot-wide chamber with a **weeping wound**: a gash in the ceiling leaks blood into a pool where a scab floats. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] walks in and is ambushed by four invisible barlguras, each popping into view as it drops down. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] and [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] land some damage. I step in and kill two, then another, then the last. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] points out the barlguras were ripping the wound open, so [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] casts healing on it and the bleeding stems.

We drop down another level into a big empty chamber with a lake of blood. At the far tunnel, two frog-like aberrations fight over a corpse. We try to sneak in, quite ungracefully, and provoke them into a battle, which we win handily with haste. The corpse has a **+1 glaive**. We take it.

Heading back east under the stained glass window, we find a chamber of treasure that looks like a night hag's hoard.

Deeper: ten small, snarling demons clawing at the rock floor, with a cloud of flies shaped like a demon barking orders at them. I drop in with a plunging attack on two. The fly-cloud demon disappears into the wall. [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah|Zaleria]], [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] and [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] fire spells down at them. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] eventually drops in and kills two. They swarm me and fail to land a hit. I kill four. [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah|Zaleria]] drops more guiding bolts, [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] follows suit, [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] drops a starry wisp, [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] bashes another. I slice the last two into ribbons.

Another tunnel opens into a massive chamber where horse-sized flies are eating a spiny devil hung by chains from the ceiling. I charge in and fire a lightning bolt from my sword. They fly at me and wound me grievously. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] runs in and heals me. The rest filter in. One drops [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] with its droning hum. I slay it with four blows, drop another a few seconds later, and [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] drops the last after I weaken it.

The devil hanging from the chains is **[[Alazub]]**, a barbed devil. He trusts [[Lulu]]. [[Trantolox]], a nalfeshnee, caught him sneaking around the scab and wanted to know whether he knew anything that would help the demons break in. [[Trantolox]] runs a crew tunneling into the citadel, trying to free a demon named [[Crokek'toeck]], and [[Alazub]] wants to stop them. We free him since our goals line up, and he's grateful.

Back in the chamber where [[Trantolox]]'s goons dug a week ago, we glimpse demons heading the same way. They notice us and run. We give chase and lose them.

## The Doors
We drop down and take the left path to a massive chamber with brass double doors bearing a relief of [[Zariel]], with gold runes inlaid in the frame. Three barb-headed demons are bashing the door while another shouts at them in Abyssal. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] casts feather fall on us all and we drop in.

I turn one into a cloud of blood mist, 16 pieces in an instant. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] and [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah|Zaleria]] press. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] and [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] send balls of holy energy. After a few rounds we stand surveying the wounds among their corpses. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] is hurt badly. Then [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah|Zaleria]] pushes the door they couldn't break, and it swings open with a slight push.
