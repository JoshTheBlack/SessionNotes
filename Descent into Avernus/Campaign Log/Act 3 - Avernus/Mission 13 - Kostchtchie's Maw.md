## Kostchtchie's Maw
We reach the arches without resistance. At the edge of the maw we jump, feather falling to the bottom. Chained in the center is [[Lord Kostchtchie|Kostchtchie]]. "Free me, bring me my hammer!" he booms. "That's precisely why we're here," I answer.

A chain demon with animated chains steps from an alcove and four hellhounds circle us. The chains burst from the ground at [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]]'s feet, trying to slash and grapple him. The hounds bite and claw. Five long rounds later, they're dead. [[Lord Kostchtchie|Kostchtchie]] breaks free, screams "ZARIEL!" and flies off.

We take the gradual climb out the other side, which means a long hike back to the grinder, around the eastern volcano. A few hours in, we run into a giant hornets' nest, and thirteen of the blasted things attack. They love fire, so we're hamstrung. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] burns them with radiant magic. [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah|Zaleria]] swings wildly and never connects. I kill the first, [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] the second, and I kill a few more. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] gets one. [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah|Zaleria]] keeps flailing; the bugs laugh at her. I kill three more and end it.

We walk another hour, rest, and make it back to the grinder. Through Ulloch's gate, thinking of the Hellknight tomb, and: "For Glory." We're back inside.

[[Olanthius]] takes the map, marks the [[The Bleeding Citadel|Bleeding Citadel]], and takes his leave, reminding us of our promise to free his companions. I use the shatter spell stored in my thunder sword to destroy one of the tablets of names. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] destroys the other three with thunderwave. We feel their spirits leave.
