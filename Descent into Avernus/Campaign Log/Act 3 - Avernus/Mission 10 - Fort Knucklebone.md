> [!abstract] The Job
> Descend into [[Avernus]], reach [[Fort Knucklebone]], and get [[Lulu]]'s memories back.

## The Fall
We dive off the edge of [[Elturel]], aiming for [[Fort Knucklebone]]. Landmarks announce themselves as we pass, whispered by the map straight into [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]]'s mind.

We land. The air smells like burning tar. The wind alternates between gale and soft breeze. Ash chokes the air, and giant flies are everywhere. Nupperibos crawl on the ground. Puddles of demon ichor dot the wasteland like the world's worst picnic.

We pick a direction and walk. The first few hours are mercifully quiet.

## The Wandering Emporium
Then, off to the side, [[Lulu]] points out [[The Wandering Emporium]], ringed with a perimeter of demon grinders. Inside, we meet its proprietor, [[Mahadi The Rakshasa]], who recommends the [[Infernal Rapture]] if we're hungry. Payment due at time of service. Alternative payment available. I do not like the sound of "alternative."

He offers to sell us an infernal machine for roughly 10 soul coins, about 1,000 gold. It runs on soul coins.

Stalls inside the Emporium:
- [[Infernal Rapture]]: a tavern.
- Firesnake Forge: weapons and armor. We sell a pile of spare gear for 10 soul coins.
- Burnie the Barber: a barber shop where you forget everything that happened after you leave. No thanks.
- From Here to Avernus: a post office.
- Ichors Away: an apothecary of ichor.
- Zeneth's Larva Farm: six large larvae in cages.

[[Thomus - Thomas]] spends the visit trying to piss off every local by asking each why they're sentenced to Hell. At the Infernal Rapture, a tall, grim, hook-nosed humanoid with spindly fingers stands at a podium and asks if we'd like to come in. The prices are exorbitant. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] and [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] order dessert. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] orders tea. I order *nothing*, and it kills me.

## The Wasteland
We walk until we can't, then camp. After a rest, we move on. The wind shifts to our tails and four lemures attack. We dispatch them and arrive at [[Fort Knucklebone]].

## Fort Knucklebone
Gnomish creatures stand sentry on the walls and heckle us for the password. I tell them it's *knuckle sandwich*. They want a secret dance. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] does the worm. They laugh, open the gate, and let us in.

Inside are [[Chukka]] and [[Clonk]]. They bring out an old hag with red-eyed ravens on her shoulders and a giant fleshy golem trailing behind: [[Mad Maggie]]. She says she can help [[Lulu]] recover her memories and goes off to prepare a ritual.

[[Chukka]] and [[Clonk]] are struggling to fix a demon grinder. [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]] studies it and finds a gear suffering from a demonic energy malady. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] casts a spell and the machine whirs back to life. [[Clonk]] dashes off to tell [[Mad Maggie|Maggie]] we're useful.

We meet the golem, hurt. [[Lulu]] discerns his name: Mickey. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] studies his wounds and heals him up a bit.

An impish interloper tells us [[Mad Maggie|Maggie]]'s ready. He turns into a raven and we follow him to her quarters.

## The Ritual
The ritual pulls us into a dream. We push through the first level and end up dream-fighting devils. When we beat them, they beg us to let them live, and they'll serve us for eternity. We spare them.

Then we wander a vast black landscape of devastation, overwhelmed with loss. [[Mad Maggie|Maggie]]'s disembodied voice: "Don't let the sludge consume you, you must push through her defenses. You must *look*." The black goop begins to envelop us. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan|Baktha]] breaks free first and floats in the air. The devils we spared return just as I break loose, taking psychic damage as despair tries to drown me. [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomas]] follows, then [[Salah Secumber - Hal|Salah]], and finally [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike|Mike]], with an assist from the devils.

The next vision: [[Lulu]] approaches a spyglass, presses her eye to it, steps back, and cries out that she knows where the sword is. We all wake.

[[Lulu]] is ecstatic. She is ripped and ready. [[Mad Maggie|Maggie]] seems deeply contented, happy to have shared that suffering with us, and rewards us with gifts. We rest and set off, promising to return and show her the sword if we can.
