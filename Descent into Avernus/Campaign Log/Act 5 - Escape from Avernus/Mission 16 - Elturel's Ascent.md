> [!abstract] The Job
> Get [[Zariel]]'s sword where it needs to go, free [[Elturel]] from its chains, and bring the city home.

## The Shield Problem
[[Lulu]], fully restored, tells us how the shield I've been hauling around can be destroyed. A good god could do it. An archdevil could do it. And some weird planetary thing or other could do it too.

Wonderful. Three options, and all of them are somebody else's problem.

## The Sword
We put [[Zariel]]'s sword in her hands, and it was enough. The angel in there answered. Her halo, her wings and her devil's face burned away, and she was *redeemed*. I've met few things in my life that deserved it less, and I stood there anyway, holding my breath like everyone else.

## The Chains
[[Zariel]] cut the chains, and [[Elturel]] rose back to the surface. We made our way into the city and rose out of [[Avernus]] with it, and that's where the story leaves us. I'd like to say I felt something. I mostly wanted breakfast.

The shield? [[Zariel]] destroyed it. [[Gargauth]] doesn't get to whisper in anyone's ear anymore.
