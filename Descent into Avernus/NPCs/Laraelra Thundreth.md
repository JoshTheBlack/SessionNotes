Owner and proprietor of [[The Low Lantern]], known as "the Captain": a middle-aged human mage with a crab familiar on her shoulder. She doesn't care what her customers get up to, or how rowdy they get, as long as they pay for the damage.

> [!info]- From the book
> She works from a cabin on the ship and spends dawn to noon resting and reading her spellbook. Her staff includes two kenku bartenders and six human bouncers.
