A celestial elephant with butterfly wings who talks like she's eleven cups of tea in and remembers about as well as a goldfish. We met her in [[Traxigor]]'s floating tower, on [[Sylvira Savikas]]'s say-so. She was with [[Zariel]] the day the [[Hellriders]] charged into [[Avernus]], and she helped hide [[Zariel]]'s sword. Everything since is fog. Pieces keep surfacing as we travel: the [[The Wandering Emporium|Wandering Emporium]], [[Fort Knucklebone]], the sword hidden in the [[The Bleeding Citadel|Bleeding Citadel]]. She can turn into a mammoth, blow a trumpet of sparkles that turns a devil into confetti, and she's the one person in [[Avernus]] I'd take a hit for. Don't tell her.

> [!info]- Lore
> Lulu is a **hollyphant**, a tiny celestial elephant with butterfly wings, once [[Zariel]]'s friend and mount. When [[Zariel]] fell, Lulu traded her memories and magic to help hide the sword. She regains her memories in steps through the adventure and, if [[Zariel]] is redeemed, regains her true form.
