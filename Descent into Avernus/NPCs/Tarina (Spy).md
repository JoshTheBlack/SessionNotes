A spy who hangs out at [[Elfsong Tavern]].

Tarina gained her wealth by stealing from her crewmates.  That crime is finally catching up to her, as she believes her comeuppance is nigh. 

#npc #friendly

> [!info]- Lore
> In the module, Tarina is an informant who offers the party information about the [[Dead Three]] at the Elfsong in exchange for protection.

> [!info]- From the book
> "Tarina" isn't her real name. Before settling in [[Baldur's Gate]] she was a pirate known as Rhonda Thunderbell (also not her real name), served Captain [[Murosko Sessprin (Captain)|Sessprin]] for months, then stole her crewmates' loot and fled. She cheats at Baldur's Bones, and is in good standing with Nine-Fingers, guildmaster of the local thieves' guild. She heard her old shipmates had tracked her to the city, and traded information on the [[Dead Three]] for bodyguards.
