Duke Dillard Porter's neice.

Veteran of Fort Beluarian in Chult, summoned home to take command of [[The Flaming Fist]] while [[Ulder Ravengard]] is missing. Gave us horses, provisions and an escort out of [[Baldur's Gate]] in [[Mission 6 - Journey to Candlekeep]], and confiscated our badges in return. Reasonable for a brass hat.

> [!info]- Lore
> She is the niece of Duke Dillard Portyr of [[The Council of Four]].
