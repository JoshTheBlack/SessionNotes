A vampire elf nailed to an iron tree, begging to be cut down. [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]]'s zone of truth made him tell us the whole sorry tale: he walked through the portal with [[Harroman]], ran back, shut it behind him, and was cursed by Lythander to suffer forever in [[Avernus]]. We cut him down and he fell to ash.
