The owner and barkeep of the [[Elfsong Tavern]]. Our notes had only [[Falten (Barkeep)|Falten]], but in the book Alan is the man who runs the place, keeps the keys, and speaks Elvish well enough to translate the ghostly song.

> [!info]- From the book
> Alan employs three cooks: a cheery halfling head chef, Chenna Fatrabbit; a testy human sous chef, Azar Valsheem; and a blind human pastry chef, Klav Martilmur. His bedroom is guarded by a rug of smothering and holds a chest with 91 gp, 176 sp and 288 cp. He remembers only the word "[[Hellriders]]" from the song.
