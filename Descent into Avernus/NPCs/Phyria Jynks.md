Keeper of the [[Creed Resolute]], the book of every Elturel citizen sworn to [[Elturgard]], hiding in the catacombs of [[High Hall]]. Timid until she saw our Torm symbols. Also known as Feria. It was her name [[Ulder Ravenguard]] kept muttering from under the helm.

> [!info]- Lore
