One of the two bird-folk tinkerers at [[Fort Knucklebone]], with [[Clonk]]. They found [[Lulu]] wandering in a delirium and helped her build her first infernal vehicle.

> [!info]- Lore
> Chukka (spelled that way in the module) and Clonk are kenku, bird-like mimics who keep Fort Knucklebone's machines running. They found Lulu wandering after the sword was hidden.
