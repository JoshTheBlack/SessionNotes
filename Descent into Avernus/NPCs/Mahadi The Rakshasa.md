The proprietor of [[The Wandering Emporium]]. Smooth, smiling, and a businessman right down to his tail. Everything has a price, and "alternative payment" is not the kind of phrase I like. He'd sell us a [[Demon Grinder]] for ten soul coins, or about a thousand gold.

> [!info]- Lore
> Mahadi is a rakshasa who runs the Wandering Emporium, a traveling market that visits every corner of [[Avernus]]. Rakshasas are fiends that disguise themselves and delight in deceiving mortals.
