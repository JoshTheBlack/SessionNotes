A gaunt man with dreadlocks and long nails, flanked by two insectoids, standing sentinel in the cemetery chapel beneath [[Elturel]]. Gruff but friendly, until the dretches showed up. His glare killed a giant scorpion outright. He serves some unnamed devil master.

> [!info]- Lore
> Gideon is a sentinel of the Elturel cemetery chapel.

> [!info]- From the book
> Gideon Lightward was a priest of Lathander who served Elturel proudly. Devils sent by [[Zariel]] posed as angels and offered him power if he'd dedicate himself to fighting demons. He gave up his sanity and free will, died when the city fell, and rose as an undead creature still serving her. His mind is broken and full of hatred, but his obsession with destroying demons means he can sometimes be made a temporary ally against the portal.
