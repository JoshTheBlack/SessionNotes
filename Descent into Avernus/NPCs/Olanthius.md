A death knight who was once a Hellrider and, deep down, still is. He killed himself rather than turn, and [[Zariel]] raised him anyway. His journals are in the [[Hellriders]]' crypt. He asked us to free his dead companions and to find [[Lord Kostchtchie]] as a distraction, and in return marked the [[The Bleeding Citadel|Bleeding Citadel]] on our map. We kept our end.
