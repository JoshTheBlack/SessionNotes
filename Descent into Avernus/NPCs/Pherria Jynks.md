Keeper of the [[Creed Resolute]], the book of every [[Elturel]] citizen sworn to [[Elturgard]], hiding in the catacombs of [[High Hall]]. Timid until she saw our [[Torm]] symbols. Also known as Feria. It was her name [[Ulder Ravengard]] kept muttering from under the helm.

> [!info]- Lore
> A priestess-guardian in the catacombs of High Hall. The Creed Resolute is the codex of citizens who took the oath of Elturgard.

> [!info]- From the book
> A lone acolyte of [[Torm]], a simple sage and expert on possession and exorcism, just starting her career. With every priest dead and no higher authority, she keeps more than a hundred frightened survivors calm in the crypts beneath [[High Hall]]. She guards the [[Creed Resolute|Tome of the Creed Resolute]]. She expected [[Ulder Ravengard]] back hours ago, and urges the party to go find him in the cemetery.
