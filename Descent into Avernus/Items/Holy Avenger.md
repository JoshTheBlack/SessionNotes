A sword of legend we found sleeping in an ossuary under [[High Hall]]. It looked mundane until a wand revealed it. [[Salah Secumber - Hal]] reconsecrated it in a fountain of holy water and gave it to [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]].

> [!info]- Lore
> A Holy Avenger is a paladin's legendary weapon. Its bearer is not threatened by evil magic and its light burns fiends.

> [!example]- Mechanics (5e)
> *Weapon (any sword), legendary (requires attunement by a paladin).* +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls. On a hit against a fiend or undead, it deals an extra 2d10 radiant damage. While you hold it, you and creatures within 10 ft have advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects (30 ft for a 17th-level paladin). Source: 5e DMG.
