An infernal war machine. We took one from wereboars and wererats after [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] shouted it to a halt. Speed 100, 200 hp, AC 19. It runs on soul coins.

> [!info]- Lore
> Demon grinders are [[Avernus]]'s infernal war machines; they run on souls.

> [!example]- Mechanics (Descent into Avernus)
> *Gargantuan vehicle (12,000 lb.), carries 8 Medium creatures and 1 ton of cargo.*
> - **AC 19, HP 200** (damage threshold 10, mishap threshold 20), **speed 100 ft.**
> - Immune to fire, poison and psychic damage, and to most conditions.
> - **Crushing Wheels:** it can move through the space of any Large or smaller creature, which must make a DC 11 Dexterity save or take 22 (4d10) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone (an extra 22 if already prone). Once per creature per turn.
> - Its weapon attacks are magical. **Prone Deficiency:** if it rolls onto its side it can't right itself and is incapacitated until flipped upright.
> - Runs on soul coins (we fed it one when it sputtered out).
> Source: *Descent into Avernus*, appendix B.
