The Frost Father: [[Lord Kostchtchie]]'s hammer, which [[Zariel]] stole. We freed the demon lord without it.

> [!example]- Mechanics (Descent into Avernus)
> *Weapon (warhammer), legendary (requires attunement).* While holding it you are immune to cold damage. Whenever it deals damage to a creature, it releases a burst of cold in a 30-ft-radius sphere: each creature in the area takes 10 (3d6) cold damage. Made by Thrym, god of frost giants. Source: *Descent into [[Avernus]]*, appendix C.
