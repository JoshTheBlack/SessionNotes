[[Zariel]]'s old angel sword, hidden in the [[The Bleeding Citadel|Bleeding Citadel]] by [[Lulu]] and [[Yael]]. Anyone who pulls it "ceases to be" and becomes something else. [[Thomus - Thomas]] volunteered.

> [!info]- Lore
> Wielding the sword changes you irrevocably into a heavenly, idealized version of yourself: lawful good alignment, Charisma 20, angelic wings (fly 90 ft), truesight, and resistance to necrotic and radiant damage.

> [!example]- Mechanics (Descent into Avernus)
> *Weapon (longsword), artifact (requires attunement by a creature the sword deems worthy).* It desires a wielder who embodies bravery and heroism. You can attune immediately, without a short rest. The first time you attune, you transform into a heavenly, idealized version of yourself and your alignment becomes lawful good. Neither magic nor divine intervention can reverse it. You gain: Celestial speech; resistance to necrotic and radiant damage; Charisma 20; feathered wings (fly 90 ft, hover); and truesight. Source: *Descent into [[Avernus]]*, appendix C.
