### Human Fighter
Distrustful of authority. Gruff, brash, occasionally offensive, quick to anger, and very fond of his food.

Level 13 Champion fighter. Born to a mother who died in childbirth and a father who left him with a tavern wench. Bounced around, landed on the streets, thieved and swindled until the army, the alternative to jail, straightened him out a bit. He still can't stand an institution. [[Campaign Background|Ten years ago]] he was *The Lookout* in the heist on [[The Low Lantern]]. Carries the [[Shield of the Hidden Lord]], a Flaming Fist badge, a thunder shortsword and a storm shortsword.
