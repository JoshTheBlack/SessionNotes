Home dale of [[Warden Morn Ingwood]], on the edge of [[Cormanthor]]. The capital is [[Highmoon]].

> [!info]- Lore
> Deepingdale is one of the Dalelands, bordering the Cormanthor forest, with Highmoon as its capital.

> [!info]- More Realms lore (Forgotten Realms Wiki)
> One of the more peaceful but strong Dalelands, with deep loyalty to the elves of Cormanthor. Governed by a Lord of Highmoon appointed by the town council. Dalefolk mostly police themselves on minor matters; serious cases go to the Lord. Laws tend to be forgiving and just.
