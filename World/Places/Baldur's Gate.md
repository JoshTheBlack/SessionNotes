The Mos Eisley of the Sword Coast, a seaside city scourged by vipers and villains. Home of [[The Low Lantern]], [[Elfsong Tavern]], [[The Bathhouse]], and the [[Vanthampur Villa]]. Ruled by [[The Council of Four]] and policed by [[The Flaming Fist]].
