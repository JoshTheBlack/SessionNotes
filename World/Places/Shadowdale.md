A town in the Dalelands, where [[Warden Morn Ingwood]] gathered the party at the [[Old Skull Inn]].

> [!info]- Lore
> Shadowdale is a small farming dale and town in the Dalelands, famous as a haven for powerful adventurers (and, in canon, the home of Elminster).

> [!info]- More Realms lore (Forgotten Realms Wiki)
> A farming community of the Dalelands, home of the archmage Elminster Aumar and of two of the Seven Sisters, Storm Silverhand and Sylune. Its many ruins make it a favorite base for adventuring companies. It was once the drow-ruled Land Under Shadow, then ruled by the water wizard Ashaba, whose name the Twisted Tower and the River Ashaba carry. Through the ages it has fought off Zhentarim and drow invasions.
