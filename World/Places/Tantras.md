The holy city of [[Torm]], where [[Offsha Di'aylen]] has been ordered to return.

> [!info]- Lore
> Tantras is a seaport and the center of [[Torm]]'s faith. (The session note spelled it 'Tantrus.')

> [!info]- More Realms lore (Forgotten Realms Wiki)
> A port city on the Dragon Reach and the capital of Vesperin. During the Time of Troubles in 1358 DR, [[Torm]] manifested here and fought the god [[Bane]] in the harbor; both gods died and the city was left partly destroyed and a dead-magic zone. [[Torm]]'s order of crusaders, the Trueblades, stayed behind to repair the damage. The city recovered, and the overhauled faith of [[Torm]] became tolerant of the other gods.
