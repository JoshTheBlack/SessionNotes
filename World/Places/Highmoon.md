The town where [[Warden Morn Ingwood]] was born, and the capital of [[Deepingdale]]. He never found it a home.

> [!info]- Realms lore (Forgotten Realms Wiki)
> A large town on the East Way, one of the most integrated settlements of humans, elves and half-elves in Faerûn after Silverymoon. Led by the Lord of Highmoon, who also rules Deepingdale. Named for Highmoon Hill, where the hero Aglauntaras built the Tower of the Rising Moon in 1022 DR. Its fortification grew out of conflict with the drow of [[Cormanthor]].
