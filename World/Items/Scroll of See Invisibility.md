A scroll found in a storeroom under [[The Broken Tower Bridge]]. [[Teager]] or [[Brok Digginstone]] could read it, since *see invisibility* is a bard and wizard spell, not a cleric one. It's in the [[Dalelands Party Inventory]].

> [!example]- Mechanics (5e)
> *Spell scroll (2nd level).* Casting it as a spell: for 1 hour you see invisible creatures and objects as if visible, and can see into the Ethereal Plane. Only a creature with the spell on its class list can cast it from the scroll. If the spell is a higher level than the caster can normally cast, the caster must make an ability check (DC 10 + the spell's level) or the scroll is lost. The scroll crumbles after use. Source: 5e DMG, PHB.
