A potion Ira and [[Thomus - Thomas|Thomus]] drank in the villa fight against ten ambushing guards. See [[Mission 5 - Beneath Vanthampur Villa]].

> [!example]- Mechanics (5e)
> *Potion, uncommon.* For 1 hour, you can use a bonus action to exhale fire at a target within 30 ft. The target makes a DC 13 Dexterity save or takes 4d6 fire damage (half on a success). You can do this up to three times. Source: 5e DMG.
