First-level cleric spell that flings a flash of radiant light at a creature. [[Warden Morn Ingwood]] opened the fight in [[Session 2]] with it, striking a mercenary square in the chest.

> [!example]- Mechanics (5e)
> 1st-level evocation, 1 action, range 120 ft, verbal and somatic. Make a ranged spell attack against a creature. On a hit it takes 4d6 radiant damage, and the next attack roll made against it before the end of your next turn has advantage, as the target glimmers with mystical light. Casting it with a higher-level slot adds 1d6 per slot level. Cleric spell list.
