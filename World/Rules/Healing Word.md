First-level cleric spell that restores a few hit points at a distance, and works as a bonus action. In [[Session 2]], [[Warden Morn Ingwood]] used it to pull [[Offsha Di'aylen]] back to his feet after the knight fell.

> [!example]- Mechanics (5e)
> 1st-level evocation, bonus action, range 60 ft, verbal. A creature you can see regains hit points equal to 1d4 plus your spellcasting modifier. It has no effect on undead or constructs. Casting it with a higher-level slot adds 1d4 per slot level. Because it is a bonus action, it can bring an unconscious ally back into the fight while you still cast a cantrip like [[Sacred Flame]]. Bard, cleric and druid spell lists.
