God of duty, loyalty and righteous courage. Patron of [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] and [[Salah Secumber - Hal]], and of the paladins of [[Elturel]]. He's supposed to be on our side.

> [!info]- Lore
> Torm the True, lawful good god of duty, courage and loyalty. His holy city is [[Tantras]]. Paladins and clerics of Torm serve across the Realms.

> [!info]- More Realms lore (Forgotten Realms Wiki)
> In 1358 DR Torm manifested in Tantras and, after discovering the crimes of his intolerant priests, punished them, then fought [[Bane]] in the harbor. Both gods were slain. See [[Tantras]].
