The Moonmaiden. Goddess of moonlight, stars and travelers, and the patron of [[Warden Morn Ingwood]]. She watches over the lost and fights Shar's darkness.

> [!info]- Lore
> Selûne is the chaotic good goddess of the moon, stars, navigation and wanderers. Her militant order is the Silverstars and her eternal enemy is Shar, the goddess of darkness and loss.
