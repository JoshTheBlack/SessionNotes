The Moonmaiden. Goddess of moonlight, stars and travelers, and the patron of [[Warden Morn Ingwood]]. She watches over the lost and fights [[Shar]]'s darkness.

> [!info]- Lore
> Selûne is the chaotic good goddess of the moon, stars, navigation and wanderers. Her militant order is the [[The Silverstars|Silverstars]] and her eternal enemy is [[Shar]], the goddess of darkness and loss.

> [!info]- More Realms lore (Forgotten Realms Wiki)
> Holds the portfolios of the moon, moonlight and stars, navigation, wanderers and seekers, and others. Most humans of Faerûn believe the moon of Toril is the goddess herself, watching over the world, with the trailing lights her tears. She appears in many forms, as the moon changes its phases.
