Treelike growths cluster along a shoreline of a lake.  Each growth is formed from the ichor of a tree sac lining the lake.  Five bearded devils heap coal around a trunk.  Four appear to be teasing the fifth.  One of the sac's falls, breaking open and birthing a Demogorgon.   We approach the devils and convince them to let us pass.

A bit further, an iron tower rises atop an island on a lake of boiling redness.  It has no windows, and the only door is high on a balcony at the top.  Red lightening crackles between two metal horns on the roof.  Standing at the base of the tower are what looks like 9 or so purple crab looking demons and three winged and horned looking demons.  

As we approach, they don't seem combative.  We start to hear their thoughts.  They wonder if we are here for work.  As I cross the bridge, a bald human looking man walks out on the balcony of the tower.  Mordenkainen is his name.  He tells us of some mage of no renown known as Ubbalux.  He tells us we will find him in the direction we are heading, when we come to an obelisk, two hills past, inside a circle of standing stones.  

Regarding the sword, he tells us we should seek the crypt of the Hellriders, east, two hills past we will find an opening to a valley.  We head toward the entrance.  On either side of the entrance, dozens of hellknight figures are kneeled around obelisks, appearing lifeless.  Just inside the entrance, a narrow hallway leads to a pair of basalt doors, relieved with images of a knight fighting a devil, both holding burning swords.  They appear sealed.  

We spend a few hours resting, dimension dooring, misty stepping, resting, and repeating to get everyone into the tomb.  At the first junction, we head right to a small room with large burial urns lining one wall.  [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] begins praying in front of one of the urns.  After a short while, some spectral apparitions appear in front of the urns.  They tell him they are part of the fallen Hellriders, and this crypt is dedicated to them.  They fought and died alongside Zariel, and they failed. Zariel and her followers turned to evil and became fiends.   Olanthius killed himself rather than turn, and Zarial raised him and turned him into a death knight.  They beg [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] to help them find rest.  They tell us to seek out Olantius' quarters in these tombs and search for evidence of his humanity to use to remind him of his humanity, that should find them rest.  They offer to stand sentinel over the entrance as we explore, and they were thankful when we relayed the story of freeing Jander and the battle with Harroman.  We learn the secret to the front door is saying "For Glory."

[[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] and [[Thomus - Thomas]] burn them to ashes, with a modicum of help from [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] and me.  

We rest up and continue exploring, eventually coming to a large, strangely shaped room with large stone slabs filled with names and frescos.  A secret door on the eastern wall opens up to a small chamber containing 8 leather tomes  on a large writing table. 

The books are journals belonging to [[Olanthius]].  He never recovered for his subjugation to [[Zariel]] and his consequent transformation into a devil.  He took his life rather than face damnation, but he was turned into an undead monster by [[Zariel]] to server her forever.  He describes [[Harroman]] as a heartless man bereft of compassion, hellbent on punishing anyone who stands in his way.  He mourns the deaths of his fallen warriors, but feels powerless to help them.  He speaks well of [[General Yale]] who he respects and secretly adores.  He hints that he knows where Yale hid [[Zariel]]'s sword, but doesn't note the location.  

As we wrap up our reading, we hear [[Lulu]]'s voice in our heads. "We've got company!"  She shares her vision with us.  There is a chariot landing outside of the tomb carrying a single rider dressed in armor and being pulled by a couple of Gorgons. As he stands, he disappears, only to apparate in the chamber among us.

He advises us the only way to do that is to seek out the demon [[Lord Kostchtschie]] who is imprisoned in Avernus.  [[Zariel]] stole his hammer, his most prized possession and the main source of his power.  As a result, he has been weakened and enslaved in a pit.  Kostchtschie's maw can be access from the arches of Ulloch and explains how the arches work.
