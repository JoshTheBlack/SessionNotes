We dive off the edge of Elturel, aiming for Fort Knucklebone.  The map calls out names of landmarks, seemingly as we pass over them, directly to [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] mind.  On landing, I notice it smells like burning tar.  The wind is variable, alternating between gale force and soft breeze.  The air is choked with ash and giant flies.  Nupperibos crawl on the ground.  There are large puddles of demon ichor scattered about.  

We set off in the direction we believe the fort to be.  Our first few hours are uneventful.  

A few hours later we notice something off to the side that [[LuLu]] calls out as [[The Wandering Emporium]].  The emporium is flanked by a perimeter of demon grinders.  We wander inside and are greeted by [[Mahadi The Rakshasa]]. He recommends we visit the [[Infernal Rapture]] if we are hungry.  Payment is due at time of service.  Alternative payment is available.   

He mentions the ability to procure an infernal machine for roughly 10 soul coins, or roughly 1000 gold.  The infernal machine runs on soul coins.  

[[The Wandering Emporium]] contains:
- [[Infernal Rapture]] - A tavern
- [[Firesnake Forge]] - 
- [[Burnie the Barber]] - A barber shop.  Strangely you forget everything that happened within after leaving.
- [[From Here to Avernus]] - A post office
- [[Ichors Away]] - Apothecary of ichor
- [[Zeneth's Larva Farm]] - 6 large larvae in cages.

[[Thomus - Thomas]] tries his best to piss off the locals by asking them all why they are sentenced to hell.  

We offer to sell some weapons and armor to the Firesnake Forge.  He buys the lot of our extra weapons and armor for 10 soul coins.  

We venture over to the [[Infernal Rapture]].  Just outside we see a podium with a tall thin grim humanoid with long spindly fingers and a hook nose.  He asks if we would like to enter the [[Infernal Rapture]].  The food prices are exorbitant.  [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] and [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] order a dessert.  [[Thomus - Thomas]] orders a tea.  I order nothing.  

We walk until we can't anymore and setup camp.  After a rest, we set out again.  A short while later, the wind shifts to our tails.  We are accosted by 4 lemures.  We dispatch them quickly, and continue on our journey, finally arriving at [[Fort Knucklebone]].

We approach, cautiously eyeing the gnomish creatures standing sigil atop the walls.  They call out to us, inquiring as to our purpose.  Laughing, they ask for the password, obviously heckling us.  I tell them the password is knuckle sandwich.  Then they ask for the secret dance.  [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] does the worm.  They laugh, open the gate, and let us inside.  

Inside, we meet [[Chucka]] and [[Clonk]].  They bring out an old hag with red-eyed ravens sitting on her shoulders and some sort of giant fleshy golem trailing behind her.  Her name is [[Mad Maggie]].  She claims she can help [[Lulu]] recover her lost memories.  She saunters off to prepare a ritual.

Chucka and Clonk seem to be struggling to fix some infernal machine.  After a bit of inquiry, we learn this is a demon grinder.  [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] studies it for a bit and determines some gear is suffering from some demonic energy malady.  [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] casts a spell and the machine whirs to life.  Pleased, Clonk runs off to tell [[Mad Maggie]] of our usefulness.

As we explore, we run into the fleshy golem.  He seems to be hurt.  [[Lulu]] is able to discern that his name is Mickey.  [[Thomus - Thomas]] studies his wounds and heals him up a bit.  

We come upon an impish interloper who tells us [[Mad Maggie]] is ready for us.  He morphs into a raven and we follow him to Maggie's quarters.  

The ritual pulls us into a dream-like state.  We push through the first level and wind up dream-fighting some devils.  When we dispatch them, the devils tells us to let them live and they will serve us for eternity.  We spare them.

We wander a vast black landscape of devastation.  We are overwhelmed with loss.  "Don't let the sludge consume you, you must push through her defenses.  You must look" [[Mad Maggie]]'s disembodied voice proclaims.  

The black goop begins to encompass us, but [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] manages to break free and floats in the air.   The devils from earlier return just as I manage to break free, taking psychic damage in the process as despair emanating from the goop tries to overwhelm me.  [[Thomus - Thomas]] breaks free next, followed by [[Salah Secumber - Hal]] and finally [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]], with an assist from the devils.  

We move along to the next vision.  [[LuLu]] approaches a spyglass, pushing her eye against it, then steps back and cries that she knows where the sword is and we all awaken from the ritual.

[[LuLu]] is ecstatic.  She is ripping and ready to go.  [[Mad Maggie]] seems very contented at the result, happy to experience that suffering with us.  She decides to reward us with gifts.  

We rest up and set off on our way, promising to return and show [[Mad Maggie]] the sword if we are able.  

About an hour out from [[Fort Knucklebone]], we see a large red dust cloud, being ridden by bore-headed and rat-headed humanoids.  It barrels toward us swiftly, like a tornado.  As it closes the gap, we notice it's an infernal machine.  

[[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] springs into action, running directly at the machine.  At the edge of his movement, he turns into a cloud of mist and barrels even closer, before solidifying with a commanding "HALT!".  The machine screeches to a halt, catapulting three wereboars and 2 wererats to the ground.  Only the driver remains seated, bashing his head in the abrupt stop, dazed from [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]]'s command.  

I rush forward and send a lightening bolt flying from my sword at the largest of the foes.  [[Thomus - Thomas]] blasts all of them on the ground with a fireball, and [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] finishes off the largest foe with three blasts from a scorching ray.  

The two wererats run up to [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] and he takes a cursed bite.  

I score two critical hits on one of the final rats, and [[Thomus - Thomas]] finishes him off with a firebolt, reducing him to ash.

[[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] finishes off driver.  The vehicle they were in is a demon grinder.  It has a speed of 100, 200 HP and 19 AC.

One of the creatures has a circlet of blasting.  

I pull out the map and we take off in our newly acquired demon grinder.  
We venture upon some giant broken chains laying across the ground just as I hear within my mind "sundered chains, as close to a place of pilgrimage as you'll find in this cursed place.  A beacon to those seeking the missing link or looking for a way out."  We notice three distinct figures standing alongside the chains a bit ahead.  

We turn around and venture outside of the fallen chains, avoiding the devils working in the distance.

After some travel, we come upon Sticks Watchtower.  "The Sticks is the demons gateway into the nine hells.  The devils keep a watchful eye on its shores"  It is a huge alabaster tower just in front of the river Sticks.    We turn to the north and continue traveling, with the river to our right.  After about an hour, our demon grinder sputters out of juice.  We feed it a soul coin.  

Harromon's hill "Behold, Zarial's dog crucified those who betrayed his mistress in battle," the map proclaims.  Wrought iron trees line a hill that leads to the summit of a steep hill.  We can see anguished knights, writhing in torment, as bloated stirges feast on their blood.

We follow those trees along and come to a narrow land bridge over the river.  The largest of the iron trees holds a crucified elf.  It is labeled [[Jander Sunstar]].  He was [[Harroman]]'s right hand.  He calls out to us.  "Please free me, please!"  He is a vampire.  Lulu tells us we can trust him after [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] casts zone of truth and questions him.

We learn that Jander and Harroman were part of the host that came through the portal to this realm.  Jander retreated back through the portal and closed it behind him.  He threw himself at the feet of Lythander who cursed him to an eternity of suffering in Avernus for his sins.  We cut him down and as his feet touch ground he crumbles into ashes.

Thomas notices something coming from the northern sky.  It seems to be [[Harroman]] riding a nightmare.  When it approaches, it destroys [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] almost instantly.  I run up and slash at the nightmare, gouging its neck deeply.  [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] steps out and shoots a lightening bolt at them.  I slay the nightmare and [[Harroman]] knocks me unconscious.  

When I come to, [[Harroman]] is dead on the ground in front of me.  [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] is still unconscious and there is a swarm of sturges quickly approaching.  [[Lulu]] is grappled by a hellwasp above me in the sky, being carried off to the west.  

We dispatch the sturges fairly quickly and lick our wounds.  We find infernal tack on the nightmare.  It looks very valuable.

We head west and come upon a giant wasp nest floating in the sky, tethered to the landscape with large iron chains.  I check the map and it warms me to stay away, lest my flesh become food for these foul insects.

Beneath the nest, a congealed mass of sticky organic matter is dripping away.  "I'm detecting something magical in there" [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] tells us.  He pulls off his boots and waddles through the goop, pulling out a Storm Shortsword.

After several failed attempts to climb the chains, [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] misty steps up into the nest.  Using [[Lulu]] as a communication conduit, he keeps us apprised of his movements.  He moves up a few levels and finds [[Lulu]], stuck in the goo coated walls.  He gets briefly distracted discovering a helm of telepathy, which he pockets stealthily.  

Returning to the business at hand, he begins hatching a plan to free [[Lulu]].  It was not a good plan.  He tries to tug her free several times, eventually attracting the attention of the hell wasps.  He thunderwaves them as they venture close to inspect, dropping his invisibility briefly before regaining it as they fling against the wall.  The blast manages to loosen Lulu just enough she is able to wriggle free.  The wasps recover and move in, swiping at Lulu, nearly killing her.

She heals herself and zips out the window, heading toward the demon grinder at top speed.  

[[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]], still invisible dives out the window and falls to the ground, light as a feather, landing gracefully on his feet.  The wasps spill out of their nest, perturbed, but seem to not notice them running away.  

As all of this happened, the rest of us worked our way back to the demon grinder.  Knowing they escaped, we pulled it behind some rubble to obscure ourselves from the wasps.

[[Lulu]], panting as she arrives back to the demon grinder, tells us the sword is either n the place where demons manifest, or the place where they are destroyed.
