With our freshly marked map leading the way, we head out in search of the Bleeding Citadel.

After a few hours journey, we arrive on the outskirts.  A great, disgusting scab the size of a large hill rises up from a stinking swamp of blood. The domed top of an alabaster temple pokes through the scab. Many black iron chains of Avernus converge on the building, attaching within the grotesque mound.

We attempt to scale the chains.  [[Thomus - Thomas]] and I succeed, while [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]], [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]], and [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah]] fall into the clotted moat.  With some struggle they swim to shore and we help them up the chain.  Inside is a cavernous system of tunnels.  

The scab on one side of this narrow passage is stripped away, partially revealing one of the citadel's stained glass windows. The opaque blue and yellow panes depict the contemplative face of a beautiful angel. 

We can't find a way to open it, so we proceed up the rope and down the fleshy tunnel. The next junction is a tunnel leading downward beneath us.  Inside are 5 goat-headed demons around a pool of blood.  [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] blasts two with a lightening bolt and I rush in screaming.  Four swings of my blades later and one lies dead at my feet.  Four more swings and another lies dead at my feet.  I kick them into the pool of blood.  [[Thomus - Thomas]] uses some lightening spell, [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah]] crits one with a guiding bolt, stealing one of my kills.  

We dispatch the rest quickly.  There is a corpse floating in the blood.  On the corpse we find a flask filled with water from the river Styx and a diamond worth 5k.

A bit further down the tunnel we find a room with a weaping wound.  In this 40-foot-wide chamber, a massive wound in the ceiling continuously leaks a stream of blood, filling the floor to form a large pool where a piece of scab floats  [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] walks in as beset by a group of 4 invisible barlgura demons, each popping into existence as they drop down and attack. 

[[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] and [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] both deal a bit of damage.  I step in and kill 2 of them.  Then another, then after another round, I finish off the last.  

We notice the gushing wound on the ceiling.  [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] mentions that we may need to heal it, since the barlgura's were ripping it open.   [[Thomus - Thomas]] casts some healing at it and the bloodflow is stemmed.

Back toward the entrance, we drop down a level and continue on into a large empty chamber, filled only with a large, lake of blood.  In the tunnel at the other side, two large frog-like abberations are locked in battle, seemingly fighting over a corpse.  We try to sneak in, quite ungracefully and provoke them into a battle.  We handily defeat them with haste.  The corpse they are fighting over has a +1 glaive.  We grab it. 

Heading back east, we pass beneath the stained glass window and head deeper into the tunnels.  

In the next chamber, we find various treasures.  It looks like a night hag horde.  

Deeper still we come upon 10 snarling demons, small, clawing and biting at the rocky hard floor. There is also a demon shaped cloud of flies barking orders at the demons.  I drop down, stabbing at two of them in a plunging attack.  The cloud of flies demon disappears into the wall.  [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah]] fires some spell down at them.  [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] does the same.  Then [[Thomus - Thomas]].  None joining me in the melee below until [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] drops in and kills two.  They whack him back and a whole lot of them try to attack me, failing to find purchase.  I dispatch four of them.  [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah]] drops more guiding bolts, [[Thomus - Thomas]] follows suit, [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] drops a starry whisp, [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] bashes another to death.

They swing wildly at me further, never finding purchase.  I slice the final two into ribbons.  

Back up, we check out the tunnels we left unexplored behind us.  One goes on for a long way into a massive chamber.  Inside are a few giant horse-sized flies eating at a spiny devil suspended by chains from the ceiling.  I charge in and send a lightening bolt from my lightening sword.  They fly over to me, wounding me grievously.  

[[Thomus - Thomas]] runs in hits me with some healing.  Everyone else filters in, pressing the attack.  One of them drops [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] unconscious with its droning hum.  I slay it with 4 quick blows.  A few seconds later, I drop another.  [[Thomus - Thomas]] drops the last after I weaken it.

The devil hanging from the chains is Alazub.  He seems to trust [[Lulu]].  He came to the scab looking for [[Trantalox]], a Nalfeshnee who came looking for Zariel's sword.  Kroteqnoeque is leading a crew of demons trying to dig their way into the bleeding citadel. He wants to ensure they don't succeed.

We free him since we have similar goals.  He seems grateful.

Heading back to the chamber we found Koteqnoeque's goons digging in last week, we catch a glimpse of some demons heading the same way.  They notice us and take off running.  We press in pursuit, but we lose them.

Once we drop down into the chamber, we take the path on the left.  Eventually we come to a massive chamber with brass double doors with a relief of Zariel.  Carved into the door frame are gold inlain runes.  Three barbed headed demons are bashing into the door while another demon yells at them in abyssal.  We all touch [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] as he casts featherfall and we drop into the chamber.

I turn one into a cloud of blood mist instantly, slicing him into 16 pieces.  [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] and [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah]] press their attacks.  [[Thomus - Thomas]] and [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] send magic balls of holy energy at them.  After a few rounds, we stand surveying our wounds among their corpses.  [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] was wounded pretty grievously.  

The door the demons were trying to break into pushes open easily with a slight push from [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah]].

A bright white light burns away the blood and grit on our clothes.  Restorative energy brings life to numb musceles as the glow softens to reveal the interior of a sun-kissed cathedral.  How light passes through the scal and into the stained glass windows is a mystery only magic can answer.  Pillars line a path to a raised dais carved with celestial runes.  Zariels sword is embedded in the dais.  

When we walk in, the room disappears from around us and turns into Idleglen with a dias holding a 30 foot tall statue of Zariel.  

We are surrounded by gnolls.  We kill them judiciously.  Then we are surrounded by dretches.  We kill them extra judiciously.  A bloodied woman crawls out from beneath a topple cart, grateful for our intervention.  Ahead a mastiff and a giant hyena are locked in battle.  We kill the hyena and the mastiff is grateful.  A serpent/gnoll demon chases a group of people.  We harm the demon judiciously, as the people flee.  He flees.  Five human guards splattered with black ichor advance at us. 

I slip around the statue and attack the demon bashing at the door.  I notice a group cowering on the other side of the nearly ruined door.  I can hear everyone else battling behind me with the guards.  A bright flash of light and a blast of heat behind me is punctuated with a deadly scream.  I don't recognize the voice.  

[[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] finishes him off and we call out to the people inside.  They let us in.  After a bit of convincing, we talk Jessa, the priest within, to head outside and restore the guards vigor.  She does.  I follow her out to stand guard as the rest of our party stays inside.  She restores them to their former glory, removing whatever curse was controlling them.  Our minds fill with some unfamiliar womans voice.  She tells us we have one hour before the really bad stuff begins.  We take the time to rest. 

A noise stirs us from our rest.  We step outside, advising the survivors to bar the door behind us.  We see six demonic doglike figures off in the distance, jogging along behind a large gorilla like demon and much larger doglike demon.  

A long and vicious battle ensues, and just as we are about to end it, a Yeenoghu charges out of the fog.  

We spend a few rounds locked in vicious battle before Zariel and Lulu, in their former, pre-fallen form arrive and shove the Yeenoghu through a portal.  [[Zariel]] asks us each if we would save everyone if it meant sacrificing ourselves.  Only [[Baktha Tassup - Ryan]] answers in the affirmative.  She bestows him with a charm of vitality, and gives [[Thomus - Thomas]] and [[Zaleria Grimwood - Hannah]] a charm of restoration.  [[Iliran Tilorri - Mike]] and I get nothing, having answered no.  They disappear through the portal and we are transported to the pure white void of the sword chamber.  [[Lulu]], in her current form are here along with one of [[Zariel]]'s riders [[Yale]].

[[Lulu]] tells us [[Zariel]] had to give her soul, transforming her into a demon.  They have kept her sword in hopes it could restore her.  

[[Yale]] asks which one of us will take the sword and cease to be.  [[Thomus - Thomas]] volunteers and grabs the sword.  As he pulls it out, a burst explodes out from where we are standing, destroying everything in our vicinity.  The shield on my back screeches, attempting to overthrow my will but failing.  

[[Thomus - Thomas]] is transformed into a lawful good, goody two-shoes.
